"BLACKJACK for B8Script, in a tweet. You start with $100 and bet $10 a hand.";
"Keys: any key to start; h to hit, any other key to stand. The dealer draws to 17.";
"Ties push. The game runs until you are broke. Cards print as A 2-9 T J Q K.";

"Variables";
"  r  random state (seeded by the time the first key takes)   b  your money";

"A hand is two cells on the stack: the sum, with every ace counted as 1, and a";
"  flag that is true if the hand holds an ace.";

"R ( -- n ): a random number 0 to 12. r = r * 75 + 74, stored back; the high byte";
"  (256// then 255&) is taken, as the low bits of such a generator are the least";
"  random; 13// leaves quotient and remainder, % ; keeps the remainder.";
[Rr@75*74+:r!256/;255&13/%;]

"C ( -- v ): deal a card. Print its letter, taken from the string by the number";
"  R gave; its value is 1 for the ace, 2-10, with J Q K and T all 10.";
[CR:"A23456789TJQK"+,'1+:10>(;10 0)]

"H ( sum ace -- sum' ace' ): hit. Deal a card; the ace flag becomes the old flag";
"  or (card = 1); the sum gains the card. The flag is parked on the return stack.";
[HC%{:1=}|{+}]

"V ( sum ace -- total ): the best total. Add 10 if there is an ace and the sum";
"  is under 12 (the flag is -1 or 0, so & 10 gives 10 or 0).";
[V{:12<}&10&+]

"S ( sum ace -- sum ace total ): show the hand's total without using it up.";
[S{:}%{:}%V]

"D ( -- sum ace ): a new hand: nothing, then two cards.";
[D0 0HH]

"Start: print the title and count while waiting for a key. The count is the";
"  seed in r. You have $100 in b.";
"BLACKJACK. Key"$0 1(1+1?0=)r!100b!

"The game is one loop, a hand a pass, that ends when the money is 0 or less.";
"  Each pass:";
"  1. Print a new line, the money and your hand (two cards).";
"  2. Your turn, a loop: show the total, and ask h? if it is under 21; h hits";
"     and goes round again, any other key ends it. At 21 or more it ends at once.";
"  3. Your final total stays on the stack, or -1 if you went over 21.";
"  4. The dealer's cards, a loop: hit while the total is under 17.";
"  5. Print the dealer's total; over 21 it then counts as 0. Subtract it from";
"     yours. Your bust is -1, so you lose even if the dealer busts too (0).";
"  6. The sign of the difference (-1, 0 or 1) times 10 changes the money. The";
"     new money is the loop's flag: while it is above 0, play on.";
1("/M/J$/b You "$
  D
  1(S:32'#21<:(;" h?"$0?104=0):(;H-1 0))
  V:21>(;-1 0)
  " Me "$
  D
  1(S17<:(;H-1 0))
  V:32'#:21>(;0 0)
  -
  :0<%0>-
  10*b@+:b!0>
)
