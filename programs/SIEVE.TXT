"Each pass finds the odd primes from 3 to 16383 by the sieve of Eratosthenes: flag i";

"  them with a watch to compare machines, builds, or a checked build with an unchecked one.";
"The algorithm is Gilbreath's, unchanged: every pass sets all 8191 flags, then for each";
"  flag still set counts a prime p and clears every p-th flag after it, in order. It is";
"  written for speed, by measuring every step on the Z80 build:";
"  - Flags are walked by address, eight at a time, so the loop is tested once in eight.";
"    A flag that is set calls P; one that isn't skips just two characters, (P).";
"  - The flags are set by cell stores of -1, two at a time, sixteen to a loop.";
"  - The first flag a prime p = 2i+3 clears is 3i+3, past the end exactly when i is";
"    2730 or more: P tests the flag's address against that point.";
"  - X counts the flags to clear, n, with one division, and clears them sixteen to a loop";
"    with no test, entering the loop part way for the n mod 16 left over (Duff's device):";
"    J pushes the loop's start on the return stack, the way ( does, and jumps to the";
"    entry, the way ] does. The loop y is written with no spaces, as J counts its";
"    characters: each clearing, :0%.p@+, is seven.";
"  - Values stay on the stack where they can: a variable costs two steps.";
"Variables: f the flags (8192 bytes on the heap: the last one is kept clear), e and g the";
"  ends (f+8191, f+8192), l 2f-3 (so that p = 2a-l for a flag at a), u minus the address";
"  of flag 2730, o -1, p the prime, w the clearings rounded up to sixteen, c the count of";
