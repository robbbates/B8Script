"SKI for B8Script. A slalom course scrolls up past your skier, V, between two poles |.";
"Keys: j steers left and l steers right. There is no title: the course starts at once.";
"One line is drawn about every 100 ms (the 99? below). You crash if the skier leaves";
"  the gap, and the score, the number of lines survived, is then printed.";
"Variables: q the skier's place in the gap (1 to 8 is safe), p the left pole's column,";
"  r the random state. The score lives on the stack, under the loop's flag.";

"S ( n -- ): print n spaces. A loop that stops when n is below 1: the : 1< makes the";
"  flag and \ breaks out if it is true; otherwise print a space (32 ') and take 1";
"  off n. The ; at the end drops n.";
[S 1( :1< \ 32' 1- 1) ;]

"Start values: the skier in the gap at 5, the left pole at column 9, r at 0.";
5q! 9p! 0r!

"Main loop: one line per pass. The score starts at 0, on the stack.";
"  The pole moves: r = r * 75 + 74. Two bits are made from r: the sign (:0< is -1 if";
"  r is negative) and, from the high byte masked to 0..31, the test byte < p. The";
"  step is (byte < p) - (r < 0), which is -1, 0 or +1, and the further right p is,";
"  the likelier a -1, so the course drifts back to the middle. p = p + step.";
"  The keys: 99? waits up to 99 ms for one. It is added into r, so your steering";
"  changes the course. j gives -1 and l +1 (:106=%108=-). Then q = q + steering - step,";
"  as the gap moved by step.";
"  Crash test: q must be 1 to 8 (:0>%9<&); if not, 0= makes a true flag and \ leaves.";
"  Draw the line: p spaces, a pole, q - 1 spaces, the V, 8 - q spaces, a pole, and a";
"  new line. Then add 1 to the score and loop.";
0 1( r@75* 74+ :r!
     :0< % 256/ ; 31& p@< % - :p@+ p!
     99? :r@+ r!
     :106= % 108= - % - q@+ :q!
     :0> % 9< & 0= \
     p@ S "|"$
     q@1- S "V"$
     8q@- S "|/M/J"$
     1+ 1)

"The end: print the score.";
