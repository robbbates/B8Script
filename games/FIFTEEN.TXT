"FIFTEEN for B8Script: the 15-puzzle, and the 8-puzzle on a 3 x 3 board. Fifteen";
"numbered tiles sit in a 4 x 4 frame with one gap. Slide them back into order, 1 to 15";
"row by row with the gap last. Keys: w a s d slide a tile into the gap. The key names";
"the way the TILE moves, not the gap: w slides the tile below the gap up, a the tile";
"right of the gap to the left, s the tile above it down, d the tile left of it right.";
"q gives up. The moves are counted.";
"Every puzzle is made by many random slides from the solved board, so every puzzle";
"can be solved (half of all the ways to lay out the tiles cannot). 80 x 24 screen.";

"How to read the notes. ( a b -- c ) is a stack effect: what a word takes and leaves,";
"the top of the stack on the right. [X ... ] defines word X; calling X runs its body.";
"Every comment is a string that is dropped at once (the semicolon), so it does nothing.";
"Short words that run many times keep their notes just before them, not inside, since a";
"comment still takes a little time to read where it runs (only in the .TXT: b8strip.py";
"takes every comment out of the .B8S).";

"The plan in short:";
"  1. The board b is one byte per cell, row by row, n cells to a row (n is 4 or 3).";
"     A cell holds its tile's number, or 0 for the gap. z is where the gap is.";
"  2. T makes one slide: it finds the tile next to the gap on the side the key names,";
"     moves it into the gap, and the gap takes its place. It says whether it could.";
"  3. S lays the tiles out in order and makes 200 random slides with T.";
"  4. A slide changes two cells, and only those two are drawn again.";
"  5. W checks for the solved order after every slide.";

"Hide the cursor while it runs: delete the next line for a terminal without ESC [?25l.";
"/[[?25l"$

"Variables";
"  b  the board, 16 bytes (9 used for the 8-puzzle)   n  cells to a row, 4 or 3";
"  q  cells on the board, n*n   z  the gap's cell   o  where the gap was before a slide";
"  m  slides made   g  how the game ended: 0 playing, 1 solved, 2 gave up";
"  k  direction of a slide: 0 w, 1 a, 2 s, 3 d   l  the last direction in the shuffle";
"  e  T's step from the gap to the tile   t  the tile's cell   u v  the gap's column, row";
"  c  key   i j  scratch   r  random state";

"Data layout. Cell i is row i/n, column i mod n, from 0 at the top left. The solved";
"board has tile i+1 in cell i and the gap in the last cell:";
"     1  2  3  4            1  2  3";
"     5  6  7  8            4  5  6";
"     9 10 11 12            7  8  .";
"    13 14 15  .";
"The four slides, as steps from the gap to the tile that moves into it:";
"  w (0)  the tile below the gap moves up:      the tile is at gap + n";
"  a (1)  the tile right of the gap moves left: the tile is at gap + 1";
"  s (2)  the tile above the gap moves down:    the tile is at gap - n";
"  d (3)  the tile left of the gap moves right: the tile is at gap - 1";
"A direction XOR 2 is its opposite (0 and 2, 1 and 3), which the shuffle uses.";

"Routines";

"M: ( n d -- r )  the remainder of n by d. Divide leaves quotient and remainder;";
"   the swap and drop keep the remainder.";
[M/%;]

"R: ( n -- 0..n-1 )  a random number below n, from the state in r.";
"   r@75*74+:r! is a linear congruential step: r becomes r*75+74 (a cell wraps at 65536)";
"   and a copy is left. The low bits of such a generator are poor, so dividing by 256";
"   and masking with 255 takes the upper byte (0 to 255) of the new state. Then the swap";
"   puts n on top for M, which reduces that byte to 0..n-1.";
[R r@75*74+:r! 256/;255& %M]
"   Exactly: for a state of 0 to 32767 this is its high byte. Above that the state reads as";
"   negative and the division rounds toward zero, so the byte comes out one more than the";
"   high byte (255 turning into 0) unless the low byte is 0. It is still a number 0 to 255.";

"K: ( -- key )  wait for a key, and stir the random state while waiting.";
"   A dummy 0 starts the loop. Each pass drops the previous test value, adds 1 to r,";
"   takes a quick look for a key (1?, gives 0 if none), and keeps a copy for the test.";
"   The loop ends when the value is not 0, leaving the key. How long the player took";
"   decides r, so the shuffle is different each time.";
[K0 1(;r@1+r!1?:0=)]

"L: new line (CR then LF).";
[L13'10']

"Screen. C: clear   Y: row column -- move the cursor   E: clear to the end of the line";
"C clears the screen and homes the cursor (ESC [2J then ESC [H; in a string, ESC is";
"written as a slash followed by a left bracket).";
[C"/[[2J/[[H"$]
"Y sends ESC [ row ; column H. 27' and 91' print ESC and [. The swap brings the row";
"to the top to print first, then 59' is the semicolon, # prints the column, 72' is H.";
"Rows and columns count from 1.";
[Y27'91'%#59'#72']
[E"/[[K"$]

"T: ( k -- flag )  slide a tile into the gap in direction k (0 to 3, as in w a s d).";
"   The flag is true if a tile moved, and 0 if there is no tile on that side (the gap";
"   is at the edge). Dividing z by n gives the gap's row v and column u (the remainder";
"   comes out on top, so it is stored first). e is the step from the gap to the tile:";
"   n, 1, -n or -1, as in the table above, and stays 0 when the tile would be off the";
"   board: w needs the gap above the bottom row (v below n-1), a needs it left of the";
"   right column (u below n-1), s below the top row (v above 0), d right of the left";
"   column (u above 0). Each key test is joined to its edge test with &.";
"   Then, if e is not 0: t is the tile's cell. The tile is copied into the gap, the";
"   tile's cell becomes the gap (0), o remembers the old gap and z moves to t.";
[T k! z@n@/ u! v! 0e!
   k@0= v@n@1-<&( n@e! 0)  k@1= u@n@1-<&( 1e! 0)
   k@2= v@0>&( n@-1*e! 0)  k@3= u@0>&( -1e! 0)
   e@( z@e@+t! t@b@+, z@b@+. 0 t@b@+. z@o! t@z! 0) e@]

"D: ( i -- )  draw cell i. On the screen, row r of cells is line 6+2r and column c";
"   takes the four characters from column 31+5c (between the bars of the frame).";
"   :n@/ keeps i and splits a copy into row and column (column on top); the column";
"   becomes 5c+31, the swap brings the row up to become 2r+6, and a last swap puts";
"   the line first for Y. Then b@+, is the cell's byte v. The gap prints four spaces;";
"   a tile prints a space, another space if it has one digit (so the numbers line up";
"   on the right), the number and a space.";
[D :n@/ 5*31+ % 2*6+ % Y b@+, v!
   v@0=( "    "$ 0) v@( 32' v@10<( 32' 0) v@# 32' 0)]

"F: draw the frame for n x n cells, from line 5 to line 5+2n at column 30, and every";
"   cell in it. j counts the frame's lines from 0: an even j is a border,";
"   +----+----+ ..., an odd j a line of cells, |    |    | .... Each line is a";
"   piece printed n times (the count on the stack, counted down by 1-: ) and dropped";
"   by the ;), then the closing + or |.";
[F 0j! 1( j@5+ 30Y
      j@2M( n@1( "|    "$ 1-: ); "|"$ 0)
      j@2M0=( n@1( "+----"$ 1-: ); "+"$ 0)
   j@1+:j! n@2*1+<)
   0i! 1( i@D i@1+:i! q@<)]

"W: ( -- flag )  true when the board is solved: cell i holds i+1 for every cell but";
"   the last. The flag starts as -1 (true) and each comparison is ANDed into it.";
[W -1 0i! 1( i@b@+, i@1+= & i@1+:i! q@1-<)]

"S: shuffle. Lay the tiles out solved (tile i+1 in cell i), put the gap in the last";
"   cell, then make 200 random slides. A direction that would undo the slide just";
"   made (k is l XOR 2) is not used, so the gap wanders further instead of stepping";
"   back and forth. A direction that is blocked by the edge (T gives 0) is not counted.";
"   The count is kept on the stack and only counted down when T moved a tile; the";
"   final ; drops it. l starts at 9, which matches no direction. If the slides happen";
"   to end on the solved board (W), the shuffle is done again.";
"   Every slide can be undone by another slide, so whatever S makes can be solved.";
[S 1(
      "Solved: tile i+1 in cell i, then the gap in the last cell, which is z.";
      0i! 1( i@1+ i@b@+. i@1+:i! q@<) 0 q@1-:z! b@+. 9l!
      "200 slides, never undoing the last one.";
      200 1( 4R k! k@ l@2^=0=( k@T( k@l! 1- 0) 0) :);
   "Again if that left it solved.";
   W)]

"A: show the count of slides, two lines below the frame.";
[A n@2*7+ 30Y "Moves "$ m@# E]

"Main program";

"Make the board (16 bytes) and start the random state at 0.";
16_b! 0r!
"Title screen and instructions. K waits for a key (stirring r); the ; drops the key.";
C "FIFTEEN"$ L L "Slide the tiles into order, 1 at the top left, the gap at the bottom right."$ L
"w a s d move a TILE (not the gap) up, left, down or right into the gap."$ L
"q gives up."$ L L
"Press a key."$ K;

"Outer loop: one game per pass. Ask the size: 3 (51) gives the 8-puzzle, anything";
"else the 15-puzzle. q is the number of cells. Shuffle (S), count no moves yet, and";
"draw the title, the keys, the frame and tiles (F) and the count (A).";
1( C "15-puzzle or 8-puzzle? (4//3) "$ 4n! K 51=( 3n! 0) n@:*q! S 0m! 0g!
   C 2 30Y "FIFTEEN"$
   3 10Y "w a s d move a tile up, left, down or right into the gap.   q gives up"$
   F A
   "Turn loop: one key per pass. 0? waits for a key and 32| folds it to lower case.";
   "It is looked up in wasd: i is its place, 0 to 3. If it slides a tile (T), count";
   "the move and draw the two cells that changed: the old gap o, which now holds the";
   "tile, and the new gap z. Then W tells whether it is solved, and q (113) gives up.";
   1( 0? 32| c!
      0i! 1( "wasd"i@+, c@=( i@T( m@1+m! o@D z@D A 0) 0) i@1+:i! 4<)
      W( 1g! 0) c@113=( 2g! 0)
   g@0=)
   "The end of a game: say how it went, two lines further down, and ask for another.";
   "121 is y.";
   n@2*9+ 30Y g@1=( "Solved in "$ m@# " moves! "$ 0) "Play again? (y//n) "$ E
K 121=)
C "Bye."$ L

"Show the cursor again: delete the next line too if the one at the start was deleted.";
"/[[?25h"$
