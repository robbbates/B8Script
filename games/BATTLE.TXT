"BATTLESHIP for B8Script, against the computer. Each side has a 10 x 10 sea and five";
"ships: a carrier (5 cells), a battleship (4), a cruiser (3), a submarine (3) and a";
"destroyer (2), each in a straight line across or down, not overlapping. You take";
"turns to fire at a cell of the other's sea, and are told a hit or a miss, and when";
"a ship is sunk, which one (a sunk ship is then shown as #). Sink all five to win.";
"Placing your ships: w a s d move the ship, t turns it, space or Return sets it, r";
"places the rest at random. Firing: w a s d move the cursor on my sea, space or";
"Return fires, or type the column letter and the row digit, as c5. q gives up.";
"Your sea is on the left, with your ships; mine on the right shows your shots.";
"o is a miss, X a hit, # a sunk ship. The screen must be 80 x 24.";
"The computer hunts at random on the cells of one colour of a checkerboard (every";
"ship covers at least one of them), and after a hit tries the four cells round it;";
"once two hits line up it follows the line both ways until the ship sinks.";

"How to read the notes. ( a b -- c ) is a stack effect: what a word takes and leaves,";
"the top of the stack on the right. [X ... ] defines word X; calling X runs its body.";
"Every comment is a string that is dropped at once (the semicolon), so it does nothing.";
"Short words that run many times keep their notes just before them, not inside, since a";
"comment still takes a little time to read where it runs (only in the .TXT: b8strip.py";
"takes every comment out of the .B8S).";

"The plan in short:";
"  1. Each sea is 12 x 12 bytes: the 10 x 10 cells inside a border, so a walk along a";
"     row or a column stops at the border with no edge tests.";
"  2. A byte holds what is in the cell, plus 8 once it has been fired at. So one byte";
"     tells water or which ship, and whether it was hit, and the tests are compares.";
"  3. Each side has a table of 6 bytes: the cells left of each ship, and in byte 0";
"     the cells left in all. A ship is sunk when its count reaches 0.";
"  4. Q picks a side: the words that draw and fire (P D F) then work on that side's";
"     sea and table, so the same code serves both seas.";
"  5. T, the computer's aim, works out its shot afresh each turn from your sea: first";
"     a line of two hits, then a hit with an untried neighbour, then a random hunt.";
"  6. Only the cells that change are drawn: the one fired at, and a ship when it sinks.";

"Variables";
"  u v  your sea and mine, 144 bytes each   e f  your table and mine, 6 bytes each";
"  w  the side the words work on: 0 yours (left), 1 mine (right)";
"  b h  that side's sea and table (set by Q)";
"  s  the ship being placed, 1 to 5   l  its length; when firing, a column letter";
"     waiting for its digit (1 to 10)   z  its step: 1 across, 12 down   a  its first";
"     cell; when firing, the cell fired at   o  placing: 1 set, 2 all set; firing: 1 fired";
"  x y  the cursor, column and row (0 to 9)   p  key   k  the ship hit (0 a miss)";
"  m  how the game ended: 0 playing, 1 you won, 2 I won, 3 you gave up";
"  g  the computer's shot   c d t  used by B and A   n q  used by W, q also by X,";
"  n t also by G";
"  i j  scratch   r  random state";

"Data layout. Cell (x,y), x and y 0 to 9, is byte (y+1)*12 + x+1 of a sea, so the";
"real cells are 13 to 130, the neighbours of a cell are 1 and 12 away, and the";
"border bytes (row or column 0 or 11) hold 6. A byte of a sea holds:";
"  0 water   1 carrier   2 battleship   3 cruiser   4 submarine   5 destroyer   6 border";
"  plus 8 once fired at: 8 a miss, 9 to 13 a hit on ship 1 to 5";
"So: below 6 is a cell not fired at yet (never the border); 8 is a miss; above 8 a hit,";
"on ship byte-8. The lengths are in the string 054332, one digit per ship (the 0";
"fills place 0). A table has the cells left in all (17 at the start) in byte 0, and";
"those of ship 1 to 5 in bytes 1 to 5.";
"On the screen (rows and columns from 0 at the top left) your cell (x,y) is at row 4+y,";
"column 5+2x; mine 40 columns on.";
"Columns are named A to J, rows 0 to 9: C5 is x 2, y 5.";

"Routines";

"M: ( n d -- r )  the remainder of n by d. Divide leaves quotient and remainder;";
"   the swap and drop keep the remainder.";
[M/%;]

"R: ( n -- 0..n-1 )  a random number below n, from the state in r.";
"   r@75*74+:r! is a linear congruential step: r becomes r*75+74 (a cell wraps at 65536)";
"   and a copy is left. The low bits of such a generator are poor, so dividing by 256";
"   and masking with 255 takes the upper byte (0 to 255) of the new state. Then the swap";
"   puts n on top for M, which reduces that byte to 0..n-1.";
[R r@75*74+:r! 256/;255& %M]
"   Exactly: for a state of 0 to 32767 this is its high byte. Above that the state reads as";
"   negative and the division rounds toward zero, so the byte comes out one more than the";
"   high byte (255 turning into 0) unless the low byte is 0. It is still a number 0 to 255.";

"K: ( -- key )  wait for a key, and stir the random state while waiting.";
"   A dummy 0 starts the loop. Each pass drops the previous test value, adds 1 to r,";
"   takes a quick look for a key (1?, gives 0 if none), and keeps a copy for the test.";
"   The loop ends when the value is not 0, leaving the key. How long the player took";
"   decides r, so the ships and the shots are different each game.";
[K0 1(;r@1+r!1?:0=)]

"L: new line (CR then LF).";
[L13'10']

"Screen. The strings do most of it: /{ clears the screen and homes the cursor, /}";
"clears to the end of the line, and /~ moves the cursor to the row and column under";
"the string's address (several /~ take a pair each, the pair pushed last first).";
"Rows and columns count from 0. Y: ( row column -- ) moves the cursor, and E clears";
"to the end of the line.";
[Y"/~"$]
[E"/}"$]

"Q: ( side -- )  work on side 0 (you: your sea u, table e) or 1 (me: v, f) from now";
"   on. :w! keeps the side; :( ... 0)0=( ... 0) is an if-else on it.";
[Q :w! :( v@b! f@h! 0)0=( u@b! e@h! 0)]

"P: ( c -- )  move the cursor to cell c of side w's sea. 36 (three rows) is added";
"   first, so dividing by 12 gives (y+1)+3, the screen row, and x+1 (on top). The";
"   column is 2(x+1)+3, plus 40 for my sea, and Y takes the row first.";
[P 36+12/ :+3+ w@40*+ Y]

"G: ( byte table -- char )  the character for a cell, from its byte and its side's";
"   table. Not fired at (below 6): a dot for water, or the ship's letter A B C S D";
"   (only your own ships are ever shown so). 8: o, a miss. Above 8: a hit on ship";
"   byte-8, # (35) if the table says that ship has no cells left, X (88) if not.";
"   The three tests can't be true together, so exactly one character is left.";
"   n and t hold the byte and the table: not i, which F loops with round D.";
[G t! n! n@6<( ".ABCSD"n@+, 0) n@8=( 111 0)
   n@8>( n@8-t@+,( 88 0) n@8-t@+,0=( 35 0) 0)]

"D: ( c -- )  draw cell c of side w's sea. :P moves the cursor and keeps c; b@+, is";
"   its byte. On my sea the byte is masked first: :7>& keeps it if it is above 7";
"   (fired at) and makes it 0 if not (a flag is all ones), so my ships stay hidden.";
[D :P b@+, w@( :7>& 0) h@ G ']

"N: ( ship -- )  print the name of ship 1 to 5. Each test copies the number; the";
"   last one takes it.";
[N :1=( "carrier"$ 0) :2=( "battleship"$ 0) :3=( "cruiser"$ 0)
   :4=( "submarine"$ 0) 5=( "destroyer"$ 0)]

"I: ( c -- )  print the name of cell c, as C5: x+1 plus 64 is the column's letter,";
"   and y+1 minus 1 the row's digit.";
[I 12/ 64+' 1- #]

"O: ( -- flag )  can the ship of length l go on side w's sea at cell a with step z?";
"   Every one of its cells, a + j*z, must be water not yet used (0). The flag starts";
"   true (-1) and each test is ANDed in.";
[O -1 0j! 1( a@j@z@*+b@+,0= & j@1+:j! l@<)]

"J: ( s -- )  put ship s in its l cells from a with step z. The ship's number stays";
"   on the stack: : copies it for each store, and ; drops it at the end.";
[J 0j! 1( :a@j@z@*+b@+. j@1+:j! l@<);]

"Z: ( s -- )  put ship s at random on side w's sea. l is its length (its digit in";
"   054332 less 48). Each try: z is 1 or 12 (2R is 0 or 1, times 11 plus 1); the";
"   place along the ship's line is 0 to 10-l, so that it fits, and the place across";
"   it 0 to 9. Across (z 1) those are x and y; down they are y and x, so a swap puts";
"   them the right way round. a = (y+1)*12 + x+1. Try again while O says no.";
[Z :"054332"+,48- l! 1( 2R11*1+z! 11l@-R 10R z@1=0=( % 0) 1+12* % 1+ + a! O0=) J]

"X: ( flag -- )  draw the ship being placed as * in each of its cells (flag 1), or";
"   rub it out (flag 0), which draws again what the sea holds there. Each cell is";
"   drawn by D first (:D keeps the cell), then, for the flag, P and a star over it.";
[X q! 0j! 1( a@j@z@*+ :D q@( P 42' 0) q@0=( ; 0) j@1+:j! l@<)]

"U: place your five ships. For each ship s: its length l, start across (z 1) at";
"   column 0 of row 2(s-1), so ships set in place without moving never overlap.";
"   Then a loop of keys. Each key rubs the ship out (0X), acts, and draws it again";
"   (1X) unless it was set. Keys above 64 are letters, folded to lower case.";
"   w a s d move it (x and y kept in 0 to 9), t turns it (z between 1 and 12, as";
"   13-z). Then it is pulled back to fit: x at most 10-l across, y at most 10-l";
"   down. Space (32) or Return (13): if O says it fits, J puts it there, o is 1, and";
"   0X draws its letters; else row 20 says so. r (114) places this and the rest at";
"   random (Z, s up to 5), draws the whole sea, and o is 2 to end both loops.";
[U 0Q 1s! 1(
      "Ship s: its length, its start (the space in 1- 2 matters: 1-2 would be read as";
      "1 and -2), across. Say which ship it is (/l prints its length), and draw it.";
      s@"054332"+,48- l! 0x! s@1- 2*y! 1z! 0o!
      20 2Y "Place your "$ s@N " (/l cells)."$ E
      y@1+12* x@1++ a! 1X
      "The keys: the cursor on the ship's first cell, a key, and the ship rubbed out.";
      1( a@P 0? :64>( 32| 0) p! 0X
         "w s a d move it, t turns it.";
         p@119= y@0>&( y@1-y! 0) p@115= y@9<&( y@1+y! 0)
         p@97= x@0>&( x@1-x! 0) p@100= x@9<&( x@1+x! 0)
         p@116=( 13z@-z! 0)
         "Pull it back onto the sea, and work out its first cell.";
         z@1= x@10l@->&( 10l@-x! 0) z@12= y@10l@->&( 10l@-y! 0)
         y@1+12* x@1++ a!
         "Space or Return: set it if it fits (and draw its letters), or say why not.";
         p@32= p@13=|( O:( s@J 1o! 0X 0)0=( 20 2Y "Ships can't overlap."$ E 0) 0)
         "r: this ship and the rest at random, then the whole sea drawn.";
         p@114=( 1( s@Z s@1+:s! 6<) 2o!
            0j! 1( j@10/ 1+ % 1+ 12* + D j@1+:j! 100<) 0)
         "Draw it again where it is now, unless it was set.";
         o@0=( 1X 0)
      o@0=)
   "The next ship, until all five are set (r sets them all).";
   s@1+:s! 6< o@2<&)]

"F: ( c -- )  fire at cell c of side w's sea. Add 8 to its byte and draw it. k is";
"   the byte less 8: 0 for a miss, or the ship hit. A hit takes 1 off that ship's";
"   cells left and 1 off the total (byte 0 of the table). If that sinks the ship,";
"   every cell of it (its byte is k+8) is drawn again, now as #.";
[F a! "Fired at: 8 more in its byte (:, fetches, the swap puts the address on top";
   "for the store). Draw it. k is the ship hit, or 0.";
   a@b@+:,8+%. a@D a@b@+,8- k!
   "A hit: one cell less for that ship (byte k of the table) and for all (byte 0).";
   k@0>( h@k@+:,1-%. h@:,1-%.
      "Sunk (no cells left): draw every cell of it again. Its byte is k+8.";
      h@k@+,0=( 13i! 1( i@b@+, k@8+=( i@D 0) i@1+:i! 131<) 0) 0)]

"The computer's aim. It looks only at what a player could know: which of your cells";
"it has fired at, which were hits, and which ships are sunk (they are shown). A";
"live hit is a hit on a ship that is not sunk yet.";

"H: ( c -- flag )  is cell c of your sea a live hit? Its byte is above 8, and the";
"   ship's count in your table (at e + byte-8) is above 0. For a byte of 8 or less the";
"   table read is meaningless, but the first flag is false, so the & makes it false.";
[H u@+, :8> % 8- e@+, 0> &]

"V: ( c -- flag )  has cell c of your sea not been fired at yet? Its byte is below 6,";
"   which leaves out the border (6) and every cell fired at (8 and up).";
[V u@+, 6<]

"S: ( t -- )  aim at cell t, if it has not been fired at and no aim is chosen yet.";
[S :V g@0=&( :g! 0);]

"B: ( c d -- )  c and c+d are live hits in a line, d 1 (a row) or 12 (a column).";
"   Walk from c along +d while the cells are live hits, and aim (S) at the first cell";
"   past them; then the same along -d. S skips a cell that was fired at (a miss, or";
"   the border), so a line already shut at one end is followed the other way.";
[B d! c! c@t! 1( t@H0=\ t@d@+t! 1) t@S
   c@t! 1( t@H0=\ t@d@-t! 1) t@S]

"A: ( c -- )  aim at a neighbour of the live hit c: right, left, below, above.";
[A c! c@1+S c@1-S c@12+S c@12- S]

"W: ( n -- count )  count the cells that may be hunted, and aim (g) at the one";
"   numbered n among them (from 0). A cell may be hunted if it was not fired at and,";
"   while q is 1, is on the checkerboard: (y+1)+(x+1) even, the quotient and the";
"   remainder of c by 12 added, 1& 0=. With n -1 nothing is aimed at, only counted.";
[W n! 0j! 13i! 1( i@V i@12/+1&0= q@0=| &( j@n@=( i@g! 0) j@1+j! 0) i@1+:i! 131<) j@]

"T: ( -- )  choose the computer's shot, g (0 until chosen).";
"   1. Lines: every live hit c with a live hit to its right (c+1) or below (c+12) is";
"      followed both ways by B.";
"   2. If no shot yet, every live hit's untried neighbours (A).";
"   3. If no shot yet, hunt: q 1 for the checkerboard, or q 0 for any cell if no";
"      checkerboard cell is left. -1W counts them, R picks one, W aims at it, and";
"      the count it leaves is dropped.";
[T 0g!
   "1. Each live hit with a live hit to its right or below: follow the line (B).";
   13i! 1( i@H( i@1+H( i@1B 0) i@12+H( i@12B 0) 0) i@1+:i! 131<)
   "2. No shot yet: the untried neighbours of each live hit (A).";
   g@0=( 13i! 1( i@H( i@A 0) i@1+:i! 131<) 0)
   "3. No shot yet: hunt, on the checkerboard while it has cells left.";
   g@0=( 1q! -1W 0=( 0q! 0) -1W R W; 0)]

"Main program";

"Make the two seas (144 bytes each) and the two tables (6 bytes each), and start the";
"random state at 0.";
144_u! 144_v! 6_e! 6_f! 0r!
"Title screen and instructions. K waits for a key (stirring r); the ; drops the key.";
"/{BATTLESHIP"$ L L
"Sink my five ships before I sink yours: carrier 5, battleship 4, cruiser 3,"$ L
"submarine 3, destroyer 2. Place your ships with w a s d, t to turn and space"$ L
"to set, or r to let me place them. Fire with w a s d and space, or type a cell,"$ L
"as c5."$ L L
"Press a key."$ K;

"Outer loop: one game per pass. Both seas: every byte 6 (border), then the 100 cells";
"0 (j from 0 to 99 is turned into a cell as in U). Both tables: the lengths of the";
"five ships from 054332 in bytes 1 to 5, and 17 in all in byte 0.";
1( 0i! 1( 6i@u@+. 6i@v@+. i@1+:i! 144<)
   0j! 1( j@10/ 1+ % 1+ 12* + :0%u@+. 0%v@+. j@1+:j! 100<)
   1i! 1( "054332"i@+,48- :i@e@+. i@f@+. i@1+:i! 6<) 17e@. 17f@.
   "Draw the screen: clear it (/{), the title, the two headings, the column letters,";
   "and ten rows of dots for each sea, each row after its digit (/i). Each string with";
   "several /~ has its pairs pushed in the opposite order: the one used first goes last.";
   "For the rows, i@4+:42%2 leaves row 42 row 2. Then place my ships at random (side";
   "1, which draws nothing) and yours with U.";
   2 42 2 2 0 2"/{/~BATTLESHIP/~Your fleet/~My fleet: your shots"$
   3 45 3 5"/~A B C D E F G H I J/~A B C D E F G H I J"$
   0i! 1( i@4+:42%2"/~/i  . . . . . . . . . ./~/i  . . . . . . . . . ."$ i@1+:i! 10<)
   1Q 1s! 1( s@Z s@1+:s! 6<)
   18 2Y "w a s d move the ship, t turns it, space sets it, r places all at random"$
   U
   18 2Y "w a s d move, space fires, or type a letter and a digit (c5). q gives up."$ E
   20 2Y E 0m! 4x! 4y! 0l!
   "Rounds: one shot each per pass. Your shot on my sea (1Q): the cursor is on the";
   "cell x,y. A digit just after a letter a to j (l is that column, 1 to 10) fires at";
   "that cell; then l is the column of this key if it is a letter a to j, or 0. w a s";
   "d move the cursor (a and d are column letters too), space or Return fires at the";
   "cursor, q gives up. A cell fired at before only gets a message; else F fires.";
   1( 1Q 0o!
      1( y@1+12* x@1++P 0? :64>( 32| 0) p! 0a!
         "A digit (48 to 57) after a column letter: the cell they name, cursor on it.";
         p@47> p@58<& l@&( p@48-y! l@1-x! y@1+12* x@1++a! 0)
         "A letter a to j (97 to 106) waits for its digit.";
         0l! p@96> p@107<&( p@96-l! 0)
         "w s a d move the cursor.";
         p@119= y@0>&( y@1-y! 0) p@115= y@9<&( y@1+y! 0)
         p@97= x@0>&( x@1-x! 0) p@100= x@9<&( x@1+x! 0)
         "Space or Return: the cell under the cursor. q gives up.";
         p@32= p@13=|( y@1+12* x@1++a! 0) p@113=( 3m! 0)
         "A cell asked for: above 7 means fired at before, so only a message; else fire.";
         a@( 20 2Y a@b@+,7>:( "You already fired at "$ a@I "."$ E 0)0=( E a@F 1o! 0) 0)
      o@0= m@0=&)
      "Say what the shot did, on row 15. If my table's total is 0, you have won.";
      o@( 15 2Y "You fire at "$ a@I ": "$ k@( "a hit."$ 0) k@0=( "a miss."$ 0)
         k@( f@k@+,0=( " You sank my "$ k@N "!"$ 0) 0) E f@,0=( 1m! 0) 0)
      "My shot at your sea (0Q): T chooses g, F fires, and row 16 says what it did.";
      m@0=( 0Q T g@F 16 2Y "I fire at "$ g@I ": "$ k@( "a hit."$ 0) k@0=( "a miss."$ 0)
         k@( e@k@+,0=( " I sank your "$ k@N "!"$ 0) 0) E e@,0=( 2m! 0) 0)
   m@0=)
   "The end: say who won on row 21. Unless you won, show where my ships were: every";
   "cell of my sea not fired at that holds a ship (byte 1 to 5) gets its letter.";
   21 2Y m@1=( "You sank my whole fleet. You win!"$ 0) m@2=( "I sank your whole fleet. I win!"$ 0)
   m@3=( "You give up."$ 0) E
   m@1=0=( 1Q 0j! 1( j@10/ 1+ % 1+ 12* + c!
      c@b@+, :0> %6<&( c@P ".ABCSD"c@b@+,+,' 0) j@1+:j! 100<) 0)
   22 2Y "Play again? (y//n) "$ E
K 121=)
"/{Bye."$ L
