"PIG for B8Script, solo. Reach 100 points in as few turns as you can.";
"Keys: any key rolls the die. h banks the points riding on the turn and ends it.";
"A roll of 1 loses the turn's points and ends the turn.";
"Each line shows the banked score, a plus sign and the points riding on this turn,";
"  then the roll (a held turn prints no roll).";
"Variables: y the banked score, s the points riding on this turn, d the die roll.";
"The number of turns lives on the stack, under the loop's flag.";

"Start: the title, and y and s start at 0.";
"PIG to 100, h=hold"$
0y! 0s!

"Main loop: one roll per pass, ending when y reaches 100. The turns T start at 0 and";
"  stay on the stack under the loop.";
"  1. A new line, and the status (//y+//s prints y, a plus sign, s).";
"  2. The die: 0 0 puts a die value and a dummy key under the inner loop. Each pass";
"     drops the key, adds 1 to the die, and wraps it to 0 when it passes 5, then";
"     polls a key with 1?. The loop repeats while no key came, so the die counts";
"     0 to 5 around until the key, and the key is what is left with it.";
"  3. 104= tests for h (hold). The die is then 1 more: d, from 1 to 6.";
"     If it was not a hold, print d (:0=( d@# 0)).";
"  4. If it was a hold, add s to y (s@& is s or 0). A hold or a roll of 1 ends the";
"     turn, the flag e: then s is cleared and the turn count T goes up by one";
"     (%1+% reaches under the flag). Otherwise the roll d is added to s.";
"  5. The loop goes on while y is below 100.";
0 1( "/M/J/y+/s "$
   0 0 1( ; 1+ :5> ( ; 0 0) 1? :0= )
   104= % 1+ d!
   :0= ( d@# 0)
   :s@& y@+ y!
   d@1= | :( 0s! % 1+ % 0)
   0=( d@ s@+ s! 0)
   y@100<)

"The end: the stack holds the number of turns. Print it.";
#" turns"$
