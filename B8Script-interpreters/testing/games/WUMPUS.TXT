"HUNT THE WUMPUS for B8Script, after Gregory Yob's original of 1973.";
"20 rooms on a dodecahedron, three tunnels each. Find the Wumpus and shoot it.";
"Keys: s shoots, m moves, then type room numbers and press Return.";
"";
"The cave has 20 rooms, numbered 1 to 20. In the cave are you, the Wumpus, two bottomless";
"pits and two rooms with super bats: six different rooms, chosen at random. Each turn you";
"either move to a neighbouring room (m) or shoot a crooked arrow (s). You are warned when a";
"hazard is in a room next to yours. The Wumpus wakes when you walk into its room or when you";
"shoot, and then it may move and eat you. You have 5 arrows.";
"";

"Variables";
"  t  the tunnels: a string of 60 letters, three per room, A = room 1 ... T = room 20";
"     The three letters at offset 3 x (room - 1) are the rooms the tunnels of that room lead";
"     to, so room 1 (letters BEH) leads to rooms 2, 5 and 8. The text is read in place: t";
"     holds the address of the string inside the program.";
"  o  12 bytes: the rooms of you, the Wumpus, two pits and two bats,";
"     then the same six again, kept for playing the same cave twice";
"     Byte 0 is you, 1 the Wumpus, 2 and 3 the pits, 4 and 5 the bats. Bytes 6 to 11 are the";
"     same six things as the cave was made, and never change during a game: O copies them";
"     back to bytes 0 to 5 to start a game again.";
"  q  the arrow's path: 6 bytes, 1 to 5 used";
"  z  arrows left   g  0 playing, 1 won, -1 lost   c  key   n  room typed";
"  i j k v f x  scratch   a d  used by N   r  random state";
"";

"Routines";

"M: n d -- n mod d (the division leaves quotient and remainder, the quotient is dropped).";
[M/%;]

"R: n -- random 0 to n-1: r = r x 75 + 74 (a linear congruential generator, wrapping at 16";
"   bits), then its high byte (r divided by 256, AND 255) taken modulo n.";
[R r@75*74+:r! 256/;255& %M]
"   Exactly: for a state of 0 to 32767 this is its high byte. Above that the state reads as";
"   negative and the division rounds toward zero, so the byte comes out one more than the";
"   high byte (255 turning into 0) unless the low byte is 0. It is still a number 0 to 255.";

"K: -- key. Wait for a key; while waiting r is stirred, so that the time the player takes to";
"   press the first key seeds the random numbers.";
[K0 1(;r@1+r!1?:0=)]

"L: new line.";
[L13'10']

"C: clear the screen, and put the cursor at the top left.";
[C"/[[2J/[[H"$]

"Q: key -- key  echo it if it is printable, and fold it to lower case. The key is copied and";
"   tested: above 32 (a space or a control code such as Return is not echoed), the block";
"   prints the copy. Then a new line, and OR 32 makes a capital letter small (S becomes s).";
[Q :32>(:' 0) L 32|]

"N: -- n  read a number typed with echo (0 if it doesn't start with a digit).";
"   -3? reads a line of up to 3 characters and leaves its address, which goes to a. The text";
"   ends with the Return (13). n is built up from 0: d is the character minus 48; if d is";
"   from 0 to 9 the block makes n = n x 10 + d and moves a on. The flag is copied so that the";
"   block uses one copy and the loop test the other: the loop stops at the first character";
"   that is not a digit. Then a new line, and n is left on the stack.";
[N -3?a! 0n! 1( a@,48- d! d@-1> d@10<& :( n@10*d@+n! a@1+a! 0) ) L n@]

"T: room k -- the room that tunnel k (0 to 2) of that room leads to. The swap puts k below";
"   the room; room - 1 times 3 is the offset of the room's three letters; adding k gives the";
"   offset of the letter. It is fetched from the string at t and 64 is taken off, so that";
"   the letter A (65) gives room 1.";
[T %1- 3*+t@+,64-]

"J: x y -- flag  does a tunnel lead from room x to room y?";
"   y is parked on the return stack with { so that it can be compared with each of the three";
"   tunnels of x in turn: } brings it back and :{ copies it and parks the copy again (the";
"   idiom for copying a parked value). The last } takes it for good. x is copied before each";
"   tunnel lookup, and the swaps keep the flags below it.";
"   Step by step: x y, then x T0(x), then flag0 x, then flag0 x T1(x), then flag0 x flag1,";
"   then flag0 flag1 x, then flag0 flag1 T2(x), then flag0 flag1 flag2; two ORs leave one.";
[J {:0T}:{= %:1T}:{= %2T}= ||]

"P: n -- the room of thing n: 0 you, 1 the Wumpus, 2 3 pits, 4 5 bats. It reads byte n of o.";
[P o@+,]

"G: the warnings for the rooms next to yours. k goes 0 to 2: v is the room tunnel k of your";
"   room (0P is your room). If the Wumpus is there say so; if a pit is (thing 2 or 3), or a";
"   bat is (4 or 5), say that. More than one warning can appear.";
[G 0k! 1( 0Pk@T v! v@1P=( "I smell a Wumpus!"$ L 0) v@2P= v@3P=|( "I feel a draft!"$ L 0)
   v@4P= v@5P=|( "Bats nearby!"$ L 0) k@1+:k! 3<)]

"U: the Wumpus wakes: 3 times in 4 it moves down a tunnel. It may find you.";
"   v is a random number 0 to 3. If it is less than 3, the Wumpus moves to the room that";
"   tunnel v of its room leads to (1P is its room; the new room is stored in byte 1 of o).";
"   Then, whether it moved or not, if it is in your room you lose: g = -1.";
[U 4Rv! v@3<( 1Pv@T 1o@+. 0) 1P0P=( "Tsk tsk tsk - the Wumpus got you!"$ L -1g! 0)]

"E: you are in a new room: the Wumpus, a pit or bats may be there. f is a flag for a bat";
"   snatch: the loop checks again, because the bats may drop you into another room that has";
"   a hazard. Each pass: f = 0; then the three checks. The Wumpus: it wakes (U, and it may";
"   eat you). A pit, only if still playing (g = 0): you lose. Bats: you are put in a random";
"   room 1 to 20, and f = 1 makes the loop go round again. The last f@ is the loop flag.";
[E 1( 0f!
   0P1P=( "...Oops! Bumped a Wumpus!"$ L U 0)
   g@0= 0P2P= 0P3P=|&( "YYYIIIIEEEE . . . fell in a pit!"$ L -1g! 0)
   g@0= 0P4P= 0P5P=|&( "ZAP--Super bat snatch! Elsewhereville for you!"$ L 20R1+o@. 1f! 0)
   f@)]

"V: move to a room next to yours. Ask for a room number (N) until J says a tunnel leads";
"   there: the flag of J is copied; the block prints the refusal; the other copy, reversed";
"   by 0=, makes the loop go round again. Then your room (byte 0 of o) is set and E";
"   checks what is in the new room.";
[V 1( "Where to? "$ N n! 0Pn@J :0=( "Not possible - "$ 0) 0=) n@o@. E]

"F: shoot an arrow through 1 to 5 rooms.";
"   First ask for the number of rooms k until it is 1 to 5. Then ask for the path. j counts";
"   1 to k and q[j] is the room j of the path (byte j of q). The inner loop asks again if";
"   j is above 2 and the room is the same as q[j-2], the room the arrow just left: the";
"   arrow cannot turn straight back. Then the flight. x is where the arrow is (it starts";
"   in your room). For each room v of the path: if a tunnel leads from x to v the arrow goes";
"   there, else it takes a random tunnel (3R then T). If it is in the Wumpus room you win";
"   (g = 1); if it is in your room you lose (g = -1). The flight stops at the end of the";
"   path or when g is not 0. If nothing happened: the Wumpus wakes (U), you have one arrow";
"   less (z), and if there is none left, and you are still alive, you lose.";
[F 1( "No. of rooms (1-5)? "$ N k! k@0> k@6<& 0=)
   1j! 1( 1( "Room #? "$ N v! j@2> v@j@2-q@+,=&
         :( "Arrows aren't that crooked - try another room"$ L 0) )
      v@j@q@+. j@1+:j! k@>~)
   0Px! 1j! 1( j@q@+,v! x@v@J:( v@x! 0)0=( x@3RTx! 0)
      x@1P=( "Aha! You got the Wumpus!"$ L 1g! 0) x@0P=( "Ouch! Arrow got you!"$ L -1g! 0)
      j@1+:j! k@>~ g@0=&)
   g@0=( "Missed."$ L U z@1-:z! 0= g@0=&( "You have no arrows left."$ L -1g! 0) 0)]

"B: a new cave: six different rooms for you and the hazards, in the kept copy (bytes 6 to";
"   11 of o). For each i from 0 to 5: pick a random room v from 1 to 20, then look at the";
"   i rooms already chosen (j from 0; j less than i is tested, and the backslash leaves the";
"   inner loop when it is no longer true). If one is the same as v, f = 1 and the loop goes";
"   round again for another v. Then v is stored.";
[B 0i! 1( 1( 20R1+v! 0f! 0j! 1( j@i@<0=\ j@6+o@+,v@=( 1f! 0) j@1+j! 1) f@)
   v@i@6+o@+. i@1+:i! 6<)]

"O: start a game in the kept cave: copy bytes 6 to 11 of o to bytes 0 to 5, and set five";
"   arrows and the playing state.";
[O 0i! 1( i@6+o@+, i@o@+. i@1+:i! 6<) 5z! 0g!]

"I: the instructions, printed as plain text.";
[I L "The Wumpus lives in a cave of 20 rooms. Each room has 3 tunnels to other"$ L
   "rooms (the cave is a dodecahedron: look at one to see how it works)."$ L L
   "Hazards: two rooms have bottomless pits; fall in and you lose. Two have"$ L
   "super bats, which carry you to some other room at random."$ L L
   "The Wumpus is not bothered by hazards (he has sucker feet and is too big"$ L
   "for a bat to lift). Usually he is asleep. Two things wake him: your"$ L
   "entering his room, or your shooting an arrow anywhere. Then 3 times in 4"$ L
   "he moves one room; if he ends up where you are, he eats you."$ L L
   "Each turn you move to a room next to yours, or shoot. You have 5 crooked"$ L
   "arrows: name 1 to 5 rooms for its path. If a room isn't next to the one"$ L
   "before, the arrow takes a tunnel at random. Hit the Wumpus and you win;"$ L
   "hit yourself and you lose."$ L L
   "Warnings, when it is one room away: Wumpus 'I smell a Wumpus!',"$ L
   "pit 'I feel a draft!', bats 'Bats nearby!'"$ L L]

"Main program";
"";
"The tunnels of the 20 rooms, three letters each (A is room 1): its address goes to t.";
"The heap: 12 bytes for o, 6 for q. The random state starts at 0 (K will stir it).";
"BEHACJBDLCENADFEGOFHQAGIHJRBIKJLSCKMLNTDMOFNPOQTGPRIQSKRTMPS"t!
12_o! 6_q! 0r!
"Clear the screen, title, ask about instructions (the key wait seeds r; Q echoes it and";
"folds it to lower case; 121 is y).";
C "HUNT THE WUMPUS"$ L L "Instructions? (y//n) "$ K Q 121=( I 0)
"c starts as n (110), so that the first game makes a new cave.";
110c!
"Outer loop: one cave. Unless c is y (same cave) make a new cave (B). Start the game in the";
"kept cave (O). Then the turn loop.";
1( c@121=0=( B 0) O L "Hunt the Wumpus!"$ L
   "Turn loop: new line, the warnings (G), where you are and the three tunnels, then the key:";
   "s shoots (F), m moves (V). The loop goes on while g is 0, that is, while playing.";
   1( L G "You are in room "$ 0P# L "Tunnels lead to "$ 0P0T# " "$ 0P1T# " "$ 0P2T# L
      "Shoot or move (s-m)? "$ 0? Q c! c@115=( F 0) c@109=( V 0) g@0=)
   "The end of the game: say whether you won (g above 0) or lost (g below 0).";
   g@0>( "Hee hee hee - the Wumpus'll getcha next time!!"$ L 0)
   g@0<( "Ha ha ha - you lose!"$ L 0)
   "Like every key after the first, this one is read with 0? : only the first key (K)";
   "stirs r, so that the random numbers depend only on how long that first key took.";
   "Ask until the key is y, n or q: the three flags are ORed and reversed, so the loop";
   "repeats while the key is none of them. The outer loop ends when the key was q.";
   1( L "Same cave (y), new cave (n) or quit (q)? "$ 0? Q c!
      c@121= c@110=| c@113=| 0=)
   c@113=0=)
"Bye."$ L
