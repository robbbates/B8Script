"NIM 3-5-7 for B8Script, in a tweet. Three heaps of 3, 5 and 7 sticks. Press a";
"heap (1 to 3), then how many to take. Whoever takes the last stick wins. The";
"computer plays perfectly: you move first and can win, but only if every move";
"leaves the XOR of the heaps at 0.";

"Variables";
"  h  a heap block of 4 bytes; byte 1, 2 and 3 are the heaps (byte 0 is unused)";
"  x  the XOR of the heaps   y  their sum (0 means all gone)   t  the computer's";
"  target size for a heap";

"X ( -- ): work out x, the XOR of the three heaps, and y, their sum. The place";
"  counts 3, 2, 1 on the stack.";
[X0x!0y!3 1(:h@+,:x@^x!y@+y!1-:);]

"Print the title. Set up the heaps: 4 bytes, then 3, 5 and 7 in bytes 1 to 3.";
"NIM 3-5-7: heap 1-3, then how many. Last stick wins"$4_h!3h@1+.5h@2+.7h@3+.

"The game is one loop, a round a pass. Each round:";
"  1. Print a new line and the heaps (a loop over 1 to 3) and the prompt ? .";
"  2. Your heap: a key, echoed; its low two bits (a 0 becomes 1) give the heap,";
"     and the heap's address and size are left.";
"  3. Your count: a key, echoed; its low four bits (a 0 becomes 1). It is taken";
"     off the heap; if that goes below 0, the heap becomes 0. Then X.";
"  4. If the sum y is 0, you took the last stick: print  you win  and break (\).";
"  5. My move. X again. For each heap, 3 to 1, the target t is (heap XOR x) + (x = 0).";
"     With x not 0 that is the classic reply; with x 0 (a lost position) the flag";
"     makes it take one stick. If heap > t and t is not negative, the move is made";
"     (the heap is set to t) and the search stops (\).";
"  6. X again. If the sum y is 0, print  I win  and the loop ends; else go on.";
1("/M/J"$1 1(:h@+,#32'1+:4<);"? "$
  0?:'3&:0=-h@+:,
  0?:'15&:0=--:0<(;0 0)%.
  Xy@0=:(" you win"$0)\
  X3 1(:h@+,:x@^x@0=+:t!>t@-1>&:{(:t@%h@+.0)}\1-:);
  Xy@0=:(" I win"$0)0=
)
