"LIGHTS OUT (random start) for B8Script. A 5 by 5 board of lights; turn them all off.";
"LIGHTS14 starts with every light on; this one starts from a board stirred while it";
"  waits for a key, and LIGHTK from one that the key picks.";
"Press a key to start: until it comes, the board is stirred. Then each cell has a key,";
"  a to y, left to right and top to bottom. A capital letter is a light that is on, a";
"  small one is off. Pressing a cell's key toggles it and its four neighbours. Capital";
"  keys work too. The board is drawn in place. It needs a console with the cursor";
"  escapes (//{ and //~).";
"Variables: a to y hold the grid (see below), z the count of lit cells made while";
"  drawing.";
"The grid is 7 rows of 6 bytes, with a dead column on the right (it is also the left";
"  border of the next row) and dead rows above and below, so every neighbour of a cell";
"  is just 1 or 6 bytes away, with no edge tests. It lives in the cells of the variables";
"  a to y, 50 bytes in a row from a's address, which every interpreter here has, though";
"  the spec doesn't promise that layout (see Don't rely on). z, past it, is free.";

"F ( addr -- ): flip the byte at addr between 0 and 1 (fetch, XOR 1, store).";
[F :, 1^ % .]
"T ( addr -- ): toggle the cell at addr and its four neighbours: F on the cell, then on";
"  the cells 1 before, 1 after, 6 before and 6 after.";
[T :F :1- F :1+ F :6- F 6+ F]
"Q ( n -- addr ): the address of cell n, 0 to 24. n 5// gives the row and the column,";
"  and row * 6 + column, plus the grid's start a, plus 6 (past the dead row on top).";
[Q 5/ % 6* + a+ 6+]

"Start: clear the screen once, then set the 50 bytes of the grid to 1, counting down";
"  from 49 to 0, so every light starts on. The border starts on too, but it is never";
"  shown or counted. The loop leaves its 0 on the stack.";
"/{"$
50 1( 1- : 1% a+ . : )

"The stir: from that 0, step 8 cells on, mod 25, and press there, until a key comes";
"  (1? looks; the key is used up by 0=). 8 and 25 share no factor, so the presses go";
"  through every cell. Presses from a board that can be solved leave one that can be";
"  solved, so every start can be won; which one depends on how long the key took.";
1( 8+ 25/%; :Q T 1? 0= ) ;

"Main loop: one press per pass.";
"  Draw: z = 0, and for each cell n from 0 to 24 (n on the stack): :5// and //~ move";
"  the cursor to its row and column; then fetch its byte b (Q ,) and add it to z. The";
"  letter is 97 + n, less 32 * b, so a lit cell is a capital. Draw it and add 1 to n.";
"  Afterwards drop n. If z is 0 no light is left on, and \ ends the loop.";
"  Else read a key with 0? (it is not echoed). 1- 31& turns it into a place from 0 to 31,";
"  so a stray key can never write outside the grid; Q makes its address, T toggles.";
"  Most keys fall on a cell. A few (z to ~, Z to ^ and : to >) fall on the dead row";
"  below and toggle one cell of the bottom row alone. Space, @, grave, ? and _ and DEL";
"  toggle nothing that shows.";
1( 0:z!
   1( :5/ "/~"$
      :: Q , :z@+ z!
      32* - 97+ '
      1+ :25< ) ;
   z@0= \
   0? 1- 31& Q T
   1)
