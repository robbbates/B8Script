"MAZE for B8Script, in a tweet. A new 19 by 9 maze every game. You are @, in the";
"gap at the top right; get out through the gap at the bottom left. When you get";
"out it says how many keys you pressed (the shortest way takes 54).";
"Keys: i up, j left, k down, l right. Needs a console with the screen escapes.";

"Variables";
"  r  random state   g  the maze: a heap block of 19 rows of 41 bytes, and a";
"     quote after it, so that g@$ prints it   x y  the cell being opened while";
"     the maze is built (odd columns 1 to 37, odd rows 1 to 17)";
"  i  your place in the block: row * 41 + column";
"  The count of keys pressed is on the stack, under the main loop's flag.";

"Print the title and count while waiting for a key; the count is the seed.";
"MAZE ijkl. Key"$0 1(1+1?0=)r!

"The maze, all walls: note the heap top in g, then allocate 779 bytes (19 * 41).";
"  For each index n, c is n mod 41, and the byte is:";
"    (c = 39) * 3 + (c < 39) * 25 + 10";
"  which is 35 (#) for columns 0 to 38, 13 (CR) at column 39 and 10 (LF) at 40.";
0_g!0 1(:41/%;:39=3&%39<25&+10+1_.1+:779<);

"Then a quote (34) after the last row.";
34 1_.

"The passages: a binary tree. Visit every cell with odd row and column, y from 1";
"  and x from 1 in steps of 2. Each cell is made a space, and one neighbour too:";
"  the one to the east if it is the top row, or if the random state is negative";
"  and x is under 37; otherwise the one to the north. So the top row opens east";
"  (its last cell opens the gap at the top right) and the right column north.";
"  (The flag & 42, less 41, is +1 for east and -41 for north.)";
1y!1(1x!1(y@41*x@+g@+:32%.
    y@1=x@37<r@75*74+:r!0<&|42&41-+32%.
    x@2+:x!38<)
  y@2+:y!18<)

"The exit: a space at the left of the last row, place 697 (row 17, column 0).";
"  Clear the screen and print the maze, then you at place 79 (row 1, column 38),";
"  with the key count 0 on the stack.";
32g@697+."/{"$g@$0 79i!

"The game is one loop, a key a pass. Each pass:";
"  1. Draw @ at your place, row i // 41 and column the remainder.";
"  2. Wait for a key. Its low two bits, 0 to 3, pick a character from the string";
"     Q'Oy: l, i, j and k give Q, ', O and y, whose codes less 80 are the steps";
"     +1, -41, -1 and +41. Another key gives one of these too.";
"  3. The step is kept only if the cell it leads to is a space (code 32); else 0.";
"  4. Wipe the @ at the old place with a space, move, and add 1 to the count";
"     (a key that bumps a wall counts as a key).";
"  5. The loop flag is the new place less 697: go on until you are out.";
1(i@41/"/~@"$
  0?3&"Q'Oy"+,80-
  :i@+g@+,32=&
  i@41/"/~ "$
  i@+:i!697-
  %1+%
)

"The end: row 20, column 0: the count of keys.";
20 0"/~Out in "$#" keys"$
