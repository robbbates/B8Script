"GREED for B8Script, after the classic Unix game. A field of 70 x 20 digits, 1 to 9,";
"and you (@) on one of them. Move in one of eight directions with the 3 x 3 square of";
"keys round s: q w e up-left, up, up-right; a d left, right; z x c down-left, down,";
"down-right. A move eats as many cells as the digit you first step onto says, in a";
"straight line: step onto a 4 and you go four cells that way. It is only allowed if";
"the whole path is on the field and not eaten yet. Each cell eaten scores 1. The game";
"ends when no direction is allowed. h shows the directions you can take, Q quits.";
"The screen must be 80 x 24.";

"How to read the notes. ( a b -- c ) is a stack effect: what a word takes and leaves,";
"the top of the stack on the right. [X ... ] defines word X; calling X runs its body.";
"Every comment is a string that is dropped at once (the semicolon), so it does nothing.";
"Short words that run many times keep their notes just before them, not inside, since a";
"comment still takes a little time to read where it runs (only in the .TXT: b8strip.py";
"takes every comment out of the .B8S).";

"The plan in short:";
"  1. The field is one byte per cell: the digit 1 to 9, or 0 once eaten. It has a";
"     border of cells that are 0 all round, so a path that would leave the field runs";
"     into an eaten cell first, and one test (is the cell 0?) covers both rules.";
"  2. U turns a direction 0 to 7 into the step between neighbouring cells, so a";
"     straight line is a cell number plus that step, again and again.";
"  3. N checks a direction: the first digit gives the length, then every cell of the";
"     path must be uneaten. J makes the move; A asks whether any move is left.";
"  4. A move rubs out the cells it eats and draws @ at the end: nothing else changes.";

"Variables";
"  g  the field, 72 x 22 bytes with the border   p  your cell   s  cells eaten";
"  k  a direction, 0 to 7 (the place of its key in qweadzxc)   c  key";
"  o  the step for a direction   n  the length of a move, 0 if not allowed   t  a cell";
"  e  how the game ended: 0 playing, 1 no moves left, 2 Q   a i u v  scratch";

"Data layout. The 70 x 20 cells sit inside a border one cell wide, so the field is";
"72 cells across and 22 down: 1584 bytes. Cell (x,y), x from 0 to 69 and y from 0 to";
"19, is byte (y+1)*72 + x+1. The border bytes, the ones in column 0 or 71 or row 0 or";
"21, hold 0, as eaten cells do. The eight neighbours of a cell are at the steps";
"  -73 -72 -71        q w e";
"   -1   .   1        a . d";
"   71  72  73        z x c";
"A path is at most 9 cells long, and it stops at the first cell that is 0, so it";
"never goes past the border, and never out of the 1584 bytes.";
"On the screen (rows and columns counted from 0) cell (x,y) is at row y+1, column x+1,";
"inside a frame on rows 0 and 21 and columns 0 and 71. Rows 22 and 23 are for the";
"score and messages.";

"Routines";

"Random numbers: n`, gives 0 to n-1. The interpreter stirs its seed while the game";
"   waits for a key (0?), so how long the player takes decides the field.";

"L: new line (CR then LF), shorter than `' since it is used often.";
[L`']

"Screen. `^ clears the screen and homes the cursor, `_ clears to the end of the line.";
"Y: ( row column -- ) moves the cursor; rows and columns count from 0.";
[Y`@]

"G: ( c -- v )  the byte of the field at cell c: 0 eaten (or border), else the digit.";
[G g@+,]

"P: ( c -- )  move the cursor to cell c. Dividing by 72 gives y+1 and x+1, with x+1";
"   on top: just the row and column of the cell on the screen, as Y takes them.";
[P 72/Y]

"U: ( k -- step )  the step to the neighbour in direction k, as dy*72 + dx.";
"   The two strings hold dx and dy for the eight keys q w e a d z x c, each as a";
"   character 49 more than the value, so -1 0 1 are written 0 1 2. A copy of k";
"   indexes the first string for dx; the swap brings k up again for dy.";
[U :"01202012"+,49- %"00011222"+,49- 72*+]

"N: ( k -- n )  the length of a move in direction k, or 0 if it is not allowed.";
"   o is the step and n the digit on the first cell of the path. If that cell is";
"   eaten, n is 0 and that is the answer. Otherwise walk the path from your cell, n";
"   steps (the count kept on the stack): each step adds o to t, and :G0= tests the";
"   new cell. An eaten cell (or the border) sets n to 0 and leaves the loop with \.";
"   Either way the count is left on the stack, so the ; after the loop drops it.";
[N U o! p@o@+G n!
   n@( p@t! n@ 1( t@o@+:t! G0=:( 0n! 0)\ 1-: ); 0)
n@]

"J: ( k -- )  move in direction k, if it is allowed. N gives the length n and sets";
"   the step o. Your cell is rubbed out first; then each of the n steps moves p on,";
"   marks the cell eaten and rubs it out too. At the end @ is drawn on the last";
"   cell, and the score goes up by n. Not allowed: say so on row 23.";
[J N :(
      "Allowed: score it, rub out your cell, then n steps, each eating a cell.";
      n@s@+s! p@P 32'
      n@ 1( p@o@+:p! g@+0%. p@P 32' 1-: );
      "@ on the last cell, and the message row cleared.";
      p@P 64' 23 1Y `_ 0)
   "Not allowed (N gave 0, turned true by 0=):";
   0=( 23 1Y "You can't go that way."$ `_ 0)]

"A: ( -- flag )  is any move left? OR together the lengths of all eight directions:";
"   the result is 0 only if every one of them is 0.";
[A 0 0k! 1( k@N| k`+ 8<)]

"H: show the keys of the directions you may take, on row 23.";
[H 23 1Y "You can go: "$ 0k! 1( k@N( "qweadzxc"k@+, ' 32' 0) k`+ 8<) `_]

"S: show the score at the start of row 22: the cells eaten and what part of the 1400";
"   that is, to a tenth of a percent. s*1000/1400 is the same as s*5/7, which stays";
"   inside a cell (5*1400 is 7000). Dividing that by 10 gives the whole percent and";
"   the tenth. The score only grows, so the text never gets shorter and needs no";
"   clearing; the keys are shown further along the row, from column 27.";
[S 22 1Y "Score "$ s@# "  "$ s@5*7/; 10/ % # "."$ # "%"$]

"F: make a new field and draw it, a row at a time, so the digits appear as they are";
"   made. First the frame's top and bottom (B), and the border rows 0 and 21 of the";
"   field set to 0 (bytes 0 to 71 and 1512 to 1583). Then for each row v from 1 to";
"   20: a is the address of its first byte, v*72 bytes in; the border bytes at a and";
"   a+71 are set to 0; then for each column u from 1 to 70 a random digit 1 to 9 is";
"   printed (the digit plus 48 is its character, :48+' keeps the digit) and stored";
"   at a+u. Each row of the screen starts and ends with a bar of the frame.";
[F `^ B 21 0Y B
   "The border rows: top and bottom.";
   0i! 1( 0 i@g@+. 0 i@1512+g@+. i`+ 72<)
   "Each row of the field: its border bytes, then 70 digits, printed as made.";
   1v! 1( v@0Y "|"$ v@72*g@+a! 0a@. 0a@71+.
      1u! 1( 9`,1+ :48+' a@u@+. u`+ 71<)
   "|"$ v`+ 21<)]

"B: print a frame line, + then 70 times - then +.";
[B "+"$ 70 1( "-"$ 1-: ); "+"$]

"Main program";

"Make the field (1584 bytes).";
1584_g!
"Title screen and instructions. 0? waits for a key (stirring the seed); ; drops it.";
`^ "GREED"$ L L "Eat your way across a field of digits. Step onto a digit and you go that many"$ L
"cells in a straight line, eating them, if all are on the field and uneaten."$ L
"Move with q w e  a d  z x c (the keys round s). h shows the moves you can make."$ L
"Q quits. The game ends when no move is left."$ L L
"Press a key."$ 0?;

"Outer loop: one game per pass. F makes and draws the field. You start on a random";
"cell, (a row 1 to 20)*72 + (a column 1 to 70), which is eaten at once (it scores";
"nothing). Show the keys and the score on row 22, and draw @.";
1( F 20`,1+ 72* 70`,1++ p! 0 p@g@+. 0s! 0e!
   22 27Y "q w e a d z x c move   h shows the moves   Q quits"$ S p@P 64'
   "Turn loop: one key per pass. The cursor is put on you, and 0? waits for a key.";
   "No case folding: Q quits, and q is up-left. The key is looked up in qweadzxc;";
   "if found, J moves that way (k is its place), then the score is redrawn. If no";
   "move is left, e is 1. h shows the moves; Q (81) sets e to 2.";
   1( p@P 0? c!
      0k! 1( "qweadzxc"k@+, c@=( k@J S 0) k`+ 8<)
      A0=( 1e! 0) c@104=( H 0) c@81=( 2e! 0)
   e@0=)
   "The end of a game: say why, give the score, and ask for another (121 is y).";
   23 1Y e@1=( "No moves left. "$ 0) "Play again? (y//n) "$ `_
0? 121=)
`^ "Bye."$ L
