"TIC-TAC-TOE for B8Script, in a tweet. An opponent that can't be beaten: the";
"computer is O and opens in the centre; you are X. Press 1 to 9 for the cells,";
"row by row from the top left. The best you can do is a draw. At the end it says";
"draw or I win. A key that is not 1 to 9 passes your turn.";

"Variables";
"  m  the board of the player to move, e  the other's: each is 9 bits, one a cell";
"     (cell 1 is bit 0 with value 1, up to cell 9 with value 256). In play m is";
"     the computer's (O) and e is yours (X).";
"  w  the board W is testing   v  a search's best score, worked out by N";
"  Scores: -1 a win for the player to move, 0 a draw, 1 a loss; lower is better.";

"V ( acc line -- acc' ): the flag acc, or (the line is wholly in w).";
[V:w@&=|]

"W ( board -- flag ): true if the board holds any of the 8 lines: the three rows";
"  (7, 56, 448), the three columns (73, 146, 292) and the two diagonals (273, 84).";
[Ww!0 7V56V448V73V146V292V273V84V]

"T ( -- ): swap the two boards, to look at the position from the other side.";
[Tm@e@m!e!]

"N ( -- move ): the search, negamax, for the player to move (the board m). It";
"  leaves the best move, a cell's value, on the stack, and its score in v.";
"  The stack under the loop is: best move, c (the cell now tried, 1 to 256).";
"  The loop goes through the cells; for a free cell:";
"    - add c to m; W tests the new m. If it is a win, the move scores the flag,";
"      -1, with no search.";
"    - otherwise v is parked on the return stack, T swaps the players, N is";
"      called (the child's best move is dropped), the score is the child's v";
"      negated, T swaps back and the old v is stored again.";
"    - take c off m. If the score is below v, it is stored in v and c replaces";
"      the best move.";
"  The loop goes on while c doubled is under 512 and v is not -1: it stops at";
"  the first winning move. v starts at 2, beyond any score. After the loop, if";
"  v is still 2 (no free cell: a draw) it becomes 0. The expression v & (v < 2)";
"  does that.";
[N2v!
  1::(
    :m@e@|&0=(
      :m@|:m!W
      :0=(;v@{TN;0v@-T}v!0)
      %:m@^m!%
      :v@<(:v!{%;:}0);
      0)
    :+:512<v@-1>&
  );
  v@:2<&v!
]

"D ( -- ): draw the board. For each cell, c = 1 to 256: before cells 1, 4 and 7";
"  (c & 73) print a new line. The character is 46 (.), plus 33 if the cell is in";
"  m (79, O), plus 42 if it is in e (88, X).";
[D1:(:73&(13'10'0)::m@&0>33&%e@&0>42&+46+':+:512<);]

"The start: you have no cells; the computer has the centre, cell 5 (value 16).";
0e!16m!

"The game is one loop, a round pass: four rounds, the count (4) on the stack.";
"  Each round:";
"  1. Draw the board.";
"  2. Your move: a key, less 48, is a digit d. If it is 0, the move is 0, and";
"     nothing is added. Otherwise a loop doubles 1 d times and halves the";
"     result: 2^(d-1), the cell's value. It is added to e. (You may take a";
"     cell that is not free; the game does not stop you.)";
"  3. The computer's move: N, then add its result to m. If W says it has a line,";
"     break (\).";
"  4. The count goes down by 1. The loop goes on while it is not 0.";
4:(D
  1 0?48-:(%:+%1-:);2/;e@|e!
  Nm@|:m!W\
  1-:
)

"The end: draw the board. A broken loop leaves the count (not 0) on the stack:";
"  then print I win; else print draw. The word is chosen, then printed.";
D" draw"%(;" I win"0)$
