"ROCK PAPER SCISSORS for B8Script, against a computer that learns. First to +5 or -5.";
"Keys: 1 rock, 2 paper, 3 scissors.";
"Each line shows your letter and the computer's (R, P or S), then the score.";
"The computer keeps a table of 9 entries, indexed by your last two moves, holding";
"  what you played the last time that pair came up. It expects the same again and";
"  plays what beats it. The table is never cleared: what memory held is its first";
"  guess, and 3//%; brings any byte into 0 to 2.";
"Variables: t the table (9 bytes), i the history, (i*3 + your move) mod 9, s the";
"  score, c the computer's move, p your move. Moves are 0 rock, 1 paper, 2 scissors.";

"Start: print the rules; get the table of 9 bytes; the history and the score are 0.";
"RPS 1 2 3, to +-5"$
9_ t! 0i! 0s!

"Main loop: one round per pass.";
"  The computer's move: read the table at t + i, add 1 and take it mod 3: that is c.";
"  The address stays on the stack. Then your key: its code minus 1, and 3, is p, and";
"  it is stored in that entry of the table.";
"  Print your letter and the computer's from the string RPS (the string's address is";
"  kept and each letter is fetched at p and c), and a space.";
"  The history is updated: (i*3 + p) mod 9.";
"  The result is (p - c + 3) mod 3: 0 tie, 1 you win, 2 it wins. :1=%2=- turns it into";
"  -1 for a win and +1 for a loss, which s@%- takes from the score s (so a win";
"  adds 1). The score is printed.";
"  The loop goes on while the score is above -5 and below 5 (-5 here is a negative";
"  number, not a subtraction).";
1( "/M/J? "$
   t@ i@+ :, 1+ 3/ %; c!
   0? 1- 3& :p! % .
   "RPS" :p@+, ' c@+, ' 32'
   i@3* p@+ 9/ %; i!
   p@ c@- 3+ 3/ %;
   :1= % 2= - s@ % - :s! :#
   :5< % -5> &)
