"NIM for B8Script. A pile of 20 to 27 sticks. You and the computer take 1 to 3 in";
"  turn, and whoever takes the last stick loses. The computer plays perfectly: it";
"  tries to leave 1 more than a multiple of 4.";
"Keys: any key to start; then 1, 2 or 3 to take that many sticks.";
"Each line shows the sticks left, your take (the key is echoed) and the computer's.";
"There are no variables. The stick count lives on the stack, under the loop's flag.";

"Start: print the title, then count up while polling for a key. 7& and 20+ make the";
"  count a number from 20 to 27, which is the starting pile n.";
"NIM 1-3, last loses. Key"$
0 1( 1+ 1? 0= ) 7& 20+

"Main loop: one round (your move and the computer's) per pass. Stack: n.";
"  Your move: new line, print n, then read a key with 0? and echo it. 3& keeps its two";
"  low bits and :0=- adds 1 to a 0, so any key gives 1 to 3. Taking it off n leaves n.";
"  If n is now below 1 you took the last stick: print that, and \ leaves the loop.";
"  The computer's move: print the text, then work out (n-1) mod 4 (4//%; is the";
"  remainder), and add 1 to a 0 as before. Print it, take it off n. If n is below 1";
"  now, it took the last stick: print that. In either case the flag left is turned";
"  by 0= into the loop's flag, so the loop ends when somebody has lost.";
1( "/M/J"$ :# " take "$
   0? :' 3& :0=- -
   :1< :( " you lose"$ 0) \
   ", I "$
   :1- 4/ %; :0=- :# -
   :1< :( " I lose"$ 0)
   0=)
;
