"QUBIC for B8Script: tic-tac-toe in three dimensions, four in a row on a 4 x 4 x 4 cube.";
"You are X and the machine is O. Type a move as three digits, layer row column, each";
"1 to 4 (other characters are passed over, so 1 2 3 works too), then Return.";
"There are 76 winning lines: 16 rows, 16 columns, 16 pillars through the layers,";
"24 diagonals of the planes and 4 of the cube. The machine wins if it can, blocks";
"you if it must, makes two lines of three at once if it can, stops you doing that";
"if it must, and otherwise scores each free cell over the lines through it: a line";
"it alone holds counts 1 3 9 for 0 1 2 of its cells, one you alone hold 1 2 6, one";
"you both hold nothing. The four layers are drawn side by side.";

"How to read this file";
"  The program is a set of words (definitions in square brackets, one capital letter";
"  each) followed by a short main program at the end. Every word has a comment above";
"  it with its stack effect ( before -- after ), the variables it uses, and a walk";
"  through its body. A comment is a string that is dropped at once, but it still costs";
"  a little time each time it runs. The machine's move (words A and N, and the loop in";
"  W) runs 76 times per move or more, so those have no comments inside their loops";
"  (N and W have none at all): everything about them is explained just before the word.";
"  In these comments a slash cannot be written, so division is spelt out.";

"Variables";
"  b  the cube, 64 bytes: 0 empty, 1 X, 2 O; cell = layer*16 + row*4 + column, from 0";
"  h  the 76 lines, 2 bytes each: first cell, step   w  the machine's scores, 64 cells:";
"     the score, + 64 for each of its lines of two, + 512 for each of yours";
"  n  a line   z t  its first cell and step   u v  its X and O cells   e  a free cell in it";
"  q k  a cell that wins, one that blocks   m  the move   g  1 you won, 2 I won, 3 a draw";
"  p  moves made   f  -1 when it is your turn, 0 when it is the machine's";
"  a d i j c  scratch   r  random state";
"  The scratch letters are used like this:";
"     d  the player who is moving (1 X, 2 O)     i  a cell number or a loop counter";
"     a  the score of the line in A, or the address of the typed line in U";
"     j  the number of the next line to store in H, or the best key so far in A,";
"        or the layer number in D, or the bad-digit flag in U";
"     c  the cell being walked along a line in A, or the digit count in U";
"     o  the ask-again flag in U (o is not in the list above, being only used there)";

"Data layout";
"  A cell is a number 0 to 63. The cube b holds 0, 1 or 2 for cell c at address b+c.";
"  Cell number = layer*16 + row*4 + column, each of layer, row and column counted from 0.";
"  So the steps through the cube are: +1 next column, +4 next row, +16 next layer. A line";
"  is four cells: first, first+step, first+2*step, first+3*step. For example step 21 is";
"  16+4+1: the next layer, row and column, so the line 0 21 42 63 is a diagonal of the";
"  whole cube. The table h holds line n at h+2n (the first cell) and h+2n+1 (the step).";
"  The table w holds one 16-bit score per cell, at w+2c, for the machine's choice.";

"Screen layout (rows and columns counted from 0)";
"  row 1        the title";
"  rows 3 to 8  the four layers side by side: layer j (0 to 3) starts at column 16*j+4.";
"               Row 3 says Layer n, row 4 has the column numbers 1 2 3 4, and rows 5 to 8";
"               are the four rows of the layer, each its number and then four dots.";
"               A cell of layer l, row r, column c (all from 0) is at screen row 5+r,";
"               screen column 16*l+2*c+6.";
"  row 11       Thinking... or I play l r c.     row 13  the prompt for your move";
"  row 14       a complaint about your move      row 15  the result     row 16  Play again?";

"Routines";

"M: n d -- n mod d. The division leaves the quotient and then the remainder on top";
"   (with the sign of n). % swaps and ; drops the quotient, so the remainder is left.";
[M/%;]

"R: n -- a random number from 0 to n-1. A linear congruential generator in r: the next";
"   r is r*75+74 (the cell wraps at 16 bits). The : copies it before r! stores it. Then";
"   a division by 256 with the remainder dropped keeps the upper part (about the high";
"   byte) and 255& makes it a byte, since the low bits of such a generator are not very";
"   random. % puts n on top, and M takes the byte modulo n.";
[R r@75*74+:r! 256/;255& %M]
"   Exactly: for a state of 0 to 32767 this is its high byte. Above that the state reads as";
"   negative and the division rounds toward zero, so the byte comes out one more than the";
"   high byte (255 turning into 0) unless the low byte is 0. It is still a number 0 to 255.";

"K: -- key. Wait for a key and return its code, stirring r while waiting. The 0 starts";
"   the stack. In the loop ; drops the old value, r is counted up, and 1? takes a quick";
"   look at the keyboard (the key, or 0 if none). The : and 0= make a flag that is true";
"   while the value is 0, so it loops until a key arrives and leaves the key behind.";
"   How long you take to press the first key seeds the random numbers.";
[K0 1(;r@1+r!1?:0=)]

"L: -- send a new line (CR then LF).";
[L13'10']

"Screen. Y: row column -- move the cursor   E: clear to the end of the line";
"   The strings do the work: in a string /~ moves the cursor to the row and column under";
"   the string's address, /} clears to the end of the line, and /{ clears the screen and";
"   homes the cursor.";
[Y"/~"$]
[E"/}"$]

"H: first step -- add a line to the table h as line number j, then count j up. The part";
"   j@:+h@+ is the address h + 2*j. The : copies it and { parks one copy on the return";
"   stack. 1+ makes address+1, where . stores the step (a byte). Then } fetches the";
"   parked address and . stores the first cell there. Last, j@1+j! adds 1 to j.";
[H j@:+h@+ :{1+. }. j@1+j!]

"N: n -- fetch line n. It sets z and t (the line's first cell and step) and u and v (how";
"   many of its four cells hold X and how many hold O). It has no comments inside, as";
"   A calls it 76 times and W up to 76, so here is how it goes, part by part:";
"     :+h@+      the address of line n in h (h + 2*n)";
"     :,z!       its first byte is the first cell: copy the address, fetch, store in z";
"     1+,t!      the next byte is the step: store it in t";
"     0u! 0v!    no X or O seen so far";
"     z@ 4       the stack now holds the current cell, and a count of 4 on top";
"     1( ... 1-:)   a loop that runs 4 times. { parks the count, } brings it back, and";
"                   1-: counts it down and copies it as the flag, to repeat while not 0.";
"   Inside the loop:";
"     :b@+,      copy the cell, and fetch what is in that cell of the cube";
"     :1=( u@1+u! 0)   if it is 1 (X) add 1 to u. The 0) leaves the contents on the stack.";
"     2=( v@1+v! 0)    if it is 2 (O) add 1 to v. This drops the contents.";
"     t@+        add the step to the cell, ready for the next cell of the line";
"   At the end ;; drops the cell and the count of 0.";
[N :+h@+ :,z! 1+,t! 0u! 0v! z@ 4 1( {:b@+, :1=( u@1+u! 0) 2=( v@1+v! 0) t@+} 1-: );;]

"W: -- flag  does player d (1 X, 2 O) hold a whole line? It tries the 76 lines with N.";
"   The flag starts as 0 under the loop. For each line n, if d is 1 it ORs in (u = 4),";
"   that is, all four cells are X, and if d is 2 it ORs in (v = 4). Then it adds 1 to n";
"   and goes on while n < 76. There are no comments inside: the loop runs 76 times.";
[W 0 0n! 1( n@N d@1=( u@4=| 0) d@2=( v@4=| 0) n@1+:n! 76<)]

"A: the machine's move, into m. This is the slow part (about 4 seconds on a 4 MHz Z80),";
"   so it has no comments inside its loops. It makes three passes:";
"";
"   Loop 1 clears the 64 scores in w to 0, and sets q and k to -1 (q: a cell where I";
"   win, k: a cell where I must block you; -1 means that none is found yet).";
"";
"   Loop 2 goes through the 76 lines (n), calls N to count X (u) and O (v), and works out";
"   a, a score for the line:";
"     u = 0 (no X, so the line is open for me): a = 1 3 9 for v = 0 1 2 (and 0 for 3).";
"          It is a character of the string 1390 at index v, less 48 (the code of 0).";
"     v = 0 and u > 0 (no O, so the line is open for you): a = 2 6 for u = 1 2 (and 0";
"          for 3), from the string 1260 at index u. Your lines count less than mine.";
"     X and O both present: a = 0, the line is dead for both of us.";
"   Then a is raised by 64 if it is my line of two (u = 0 and v = 2) and by 512 if it is";
"   your line of two (v = 0 and u = 2). The three kinds of score do not mix in a cell,";
"   because the plain scores in one cell total at most 63 (a cell is on at most 7 lines,";
"   each worth at most 9): bits 0 to 5 are the base score, bits 6 to 8 count my lines of";
"   two (at most 7), and bit 9 upward count yours.";
"   Then the four cells of the line are walked from z in steps of t (c is the cell), and";
"   for every EMPTY cell, it is remembered in e and a is added to its score in w.";
"   Finally the line is looked at for three of a kind: with no X and three O (u = 0,";
"   v = 3) the one empty cell e wins me the game, so q = e. With no O and three X (v = 0,";
"   u = 3) I must block it, so k = e.";
"";
"   After the loop: if q was found, m = q and A returns at once. If not, and k was found,";
"   m = k and it returns. The idiom is q@-1>:( q@m! 0)\ : the flag (q > -1) is copied,";
"   the block sets m when the copy is true, and the \ leaves the word if the flag that";
"   is left is true.";
"";
"   Loop 3 picks the best empty cell. j holds the best key so far (-1) and i is the cell.";
"   For each empty cell it builds a key from the cell's score z in w:";
"     z@63&8*      the base score times 8, to leave room below for a random number";
"     8R+          plus a random 0 to 7, so that equal scores are decided by chance";
"     z@ 64 divide, drop the remainder, 7&, 1>, 4096&, +   plus 4096 if the cell is on 2";
"                  or more of my lines of two (taking it makes two lines of three at";
"                  once: a fork). Dividing z by 64 (and keeping the quotient) and then 7&";
"                  is the count of my lines of two. 1> makes a flag, and flag 4096& is";
"                  the branchless select: 4096 if true, else 0.";
"     z@ 512 divide, drop the remainder, 1>, 2048&, +   plus 2048 if the cell is on 2 or";
"                  more of your lines of two (I take it, so that you cannot fork there)";
"   Then :j@>( :j! i@m! 0); keeps the key if it beats j: it stores the key in j and the";
"   cell in m, and the ; drops the key either way. So the order of priority is: win,";
"   block, my fork, stop your fork, and then the best score with a little chance.";
[A 0i! 1( 0i@:+w@+! i@1+:i! 64<) -1q! -1k!
   "Loop 1 is done: all scores are 0 and q and k are -1. Next, loop 2 goes over the lines.";
   0n! 1( n@N 0a! u@0=( "1390"v@+,48-a! 0) v@0= u@0>&( "1260"u@+,48-a! 0)
      a@ u@0= v@2=&64& + v@0= u@2=&512& + a!
      z@c! 4 1( c@b@+,0=( c@e! c@:+w@+ :@a@+%! 0) c@t@+c! 1-: );
      u@0= v@3=&( e@q! 0) v@0= u@3=&( e@k! 0)
   n@1+:n! 76<)
   "Loop 2 is done. A win comes first, then a block: return at once if one is found.";
   q@-1>:( q@m! 0)\ k@-1>:( k@m! 0)\
   "Nothing is forced, so loop 3 picks the empty cell with the best key.";
   -1j! 0i! 1( i@b@+,0=( i@:+w@+@:z! 63&8* 8R+ z@64/;7&1>4096&+ z@512/;1>2048&+
      :j@>( :j! i@m! 0); 0) i@1+:i! 64<)]

"P: cell -- move the cursor to the cell on the screen (see the screen layout above).";
"   Dividing by 16 splits the cell into the layer and the rest, dividing the rest by 4";
"   splits it into row and column: the stack holds layer row column. 2* doubles the";
"   column, and { parks it. Then % brings the layer up, 16* multiplies it, } fetches the";
"   column back and + adds it, and 6+ makes the screen column. Last % 5+ % adds 5 to the";
"   row (under the column), which gives the screen row, and Y moves the cursor.";
[P 16/ 4/ 2*{ %16* }+ 6+ %5+% Y]

"Q: cell -- print it as layer row column, each counted from 1, with spaces between.";
"   Divide by 16 for the layer and the rest; % 1+# prints layer+1; 32' is a space. Then";
"   divide the rest by 4 for row and column: % 1+# prints row+1, a space, and 1+# prints";
"   column+1.";
[Q 16/ % 1+# 32' 4/ % 1+# 32' 1+#]

"S: put player d's piece in cell m and draw it. The cube b gets d (1 or 2) at b+m, the";
"   cursor goes to the cell, and the character printed is the one of .XO at index d.";
[S d@m@b@+. m@P ".XO"d@+,']

"U: read your move into m: the first three digits typed, each 1 to 4, a free cell.";
"   a is the address of the typed line, c how many digits were read, m the cell as it is";
"   worked out, j a flag for a digit outside 1 to 4, o a flag to ask again, i the digit";
"   just read. The outer loop repeats while o is true (the last o@ before the final ).";
[U 1( 13 2Y "Your move (layer row column): "$ E -8?a! 0c! 0m! 0j!
      "-8? reads a line of at most 8 characters, with echo, and a is its address. It ends";
      "with 13 (CR). The inner loop goes over the characters. a@,13= is true at Return and";
      "c@2>| is true once three digits are in. 0= makes a flag that is true to go on, and";
      ": copies it so that it is the loop flag at the final ) after the block ends with 0).";
      1( a@,13= c@2>| 0=:( a@,48- :i! -1> i@10<&(
         "A digit (0 to 9): if it is not 1 to 4 set j. Then m is changed to m*4 + digit - 1,";
         "a base 4 number: layer*16 + row*4 + column when three digits are in. Count it in c.";
         i@1< i@4>|( 1j! 0) m@4*i@+1-m! c@1+c! 0)
         "Other characters do nothing. In both cases go on to the next character.";
         a@1+a! 0) )
      "Now check. Row 14 is cleared first. It is a fault if there are fewer than three";
      "digits or if j is set: then say so and set o, to ask again.";
      14 2Y E 0o! c@3< j@|( "Type three digits, each 1 to 4: layer, row, column."$ 1o! 0)
      "If all is well so far (o is 0) and the cell is taken (b+m is not 0), complain.";
      "m@b@+,0=0= is a flag that is true when the cell holds a piece. (If o is 1, m may be";
      "nonsense, but the read is harmless and the result is not used.)";
      o@0= m@b@+,0=0= &( "That cell is taken."$ 1o! 0)
   "Repeat while o is set: o is left on the stack as the flag for the outer loop.";
   o@)]

"D: draw the empty cube. Clear the screen and print the title in row 1 (/{ then /~ in";
"   one string). Then for each layer j (0 to 3): the heading Layer n in row 3 at column";
"   16*j+4, the numbers 1 2 3 4 in row 4 at column 16*j+6, and for each of four rows i";
"   (screen rows 5 to 8) its number at column 16*j+4 and then the text space-dot four";
"   times, so the dots fall at columns 6, 8, 10 and 12 (plus 16*j).";
[D 1 2"/{/~QUBIC   four in a row on a 4 x 4 x 4 cube   you are X, I am O"$
   0j! 1( 3 j@16*4+Y "Layer "$ j@1+# 4 j@16*6+Y "1 2 3 4"$
      0i! 1( i@5+ j@16*4+Y i@1+# " . . . ."$ i@1+:i! 4<) j@1+:j! 4<)]

"Main program";

"Allocate the three tables. _ takes the bytes and leaves their address: 64 bytes for the";
"cube, 152 for the 76 lines (2 bytes each) and 128 for the 64 scores (2 bytes each).";
"Then 0r! gives the random number state a first value (K stirs it later).";
64_b! 152_h! 128_w! 0r!

"Make the 76 lines. H adds one line (first cell, step) and j counts them, from 0.";
"The first loop runs i from 0 to 15 and makes 3 lines each time (48 in all):";
"  i@4* 1H             rows: first cell 4*i, step 1. There are 16 rows (layer*4 + row).";
"  columns (the second command): i divided by 4 is the layer, times 16 is its first cell,";
"      i@4M is the column (i modulo 4), and step 4 goes down the rows.";
"  i@16H               pillars: first cell i, step 16 goes through the four layers.";
0j! 0i! 1( i@4* 1H  i@4/;16* i@4M+ 4H  i@16H  i@1+:i! 16<)

"The second loop runs i from 0 to 3 and makes 6 lines each time (24 in all):";
"  i@16* 5H   i@16*3+ 3H   the two diagonals of layer i (cells 0 5 10 15, and 3 6 9 12)";
"  i@4* 17H   i@4*3+ 15H   diagonals in the plane of layer and column, at row i: step 17";
"                          is 16+1, the next layer and the next column, and step 15 is";
"                          16-1, the next layer and the previous column";
"  i@20H   i@12+ 12H       diagonals in the plane of layer and row, at column i: step 20";
"                          is 16+4, the next layer and next row, and step 12 is 16-4,";
"                          the next layer and the previous row";
0i! 1( i@16* 5H  i@16*3+ 3H  i@4* 17H  i@4*3+ 15H  i@20H  i@12+ 12H  i@1+:i! 4<)

"Last, the 4 diagonals of the whole cube, one from each corner of layer 0 to the far";
"corner of layer 3: step 21 (16+4+1) from cell 0, step 19 (16+4-1) from cell 3, step 13";
"(16-4+1) from cell 12 and step 11 (16-4-1) from cell 15. That makes 48 + 24 + 4 = 76.";
0 21H 3 19H 12 13H 15 11H

"The introduction: clear the screen (/{) and explain the game.";
"/{QUBIC"$ L L "Four in a row on a 4 x 4 x 4 cube: along a row, a column, a pillar through"$ L
"the four layers, or a diagonal of any plane or of the whole cube (76 lines)."$ L
"Type each move as layer row column, three digits from 1 to 4, and Return."$ L L

"The outer loop plays one game on each pass, and repeats while you answer y to the";
"question at the end. First ask who starts. K waits for the key. If it is a printable";
"character (above 32) it is echoed: :32>(:' 0) copies the key for the test and the";
"emit. 32| turns a capital into a small letter, 121= is true for y (a flag, -1) and f";
"keeps it: f is -1 when you start, and 0 when the machine does. (In the string, two";
"slashes print as one slash, so the prompt reads y or n in brackets.)";
1( "Do you want to go first? (y//n) "$ K :32>(:' 0) 32| 121= f!
   "Clear the cube to empty, p = 0 moves so far, g = 0 (not over), and draw the layers.";
   0i! 1( 0i@b@+. i@1+:i! 64<) 0p! 0g! D
   "The game loop plays one move on each pass, you and the machine in turn. The first";
   "part is an if-else, f@:( then 0)0=( else 0). If f is true, U reads your move into m.";
   "Else the machine says Thinking... in row 11, E clears the rest of the line, and A";
   "finds its move in m.";
   1( f@:( U 0)0=( 11 2Y "Thinking..."$ E A 0)
      "d is the player: 2 + f is 1 (X) when f is -1, and 2 (O) when f is 0. S plays m for";
      "d and draws it, and p counts the moves.";
      2f@+d! S p@1+p!
      "Did d complete a line? Then g = d (1 you won, 2 I won). If 64 moves have been made";
      "and nobody has won, g = 3 means a draw.";
      W( d@g! 0) p@63>g@0=&( 3g! 0)
      "If it was the machine's move, say what it played in row 11, over Thinking...";
      f@0=( 11 2Y "I play "$ m@Q "."$ E 0)
      "Hand over the turn: f becomes 0 if it was -1, and -1 if it was 0. Go on while g = 0.";
      f@0=f!
   g@0=)
   "The game is over. Row 15 shows the result and row 16 asks again: y plays again.";
   "The screen is cleared after the answer, so the next question starts at the top";
   "instead of running on after this one.";
   15 2Y g@1=( "You win!"$ 0) g@2=( "I win!"$ 0) g@3=( "A draw."$ 0) E
   16 2Y "Play again? (y//n) "$ K 121= "/{"$)
L "Bye."$ L
