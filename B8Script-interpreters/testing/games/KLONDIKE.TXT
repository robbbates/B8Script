"KLONDIKE solitaire for B8Script: build the four foundations up by suit, ace to king.";
"The tableau's seven piles build down in alternate colours; only a king goes on an";
"empty pile. Draw one or three cards at a time from the stock, as chosen at the start;";
"the waste goes back to the stock when the stock is empty, as often as you like.";
"Keys: d draws. A move is a source, then a destination: w the waste or 1-7 a pile,";
"then 1-7 a pile or f the foundation (the right one is found). Moving from a pile";
"moves the deepest face-up card that fits, with the cards on it. q quits.";
"Cards are two characters: rank A 2-9 T J Q K, suit S h d C; ## is face down.";

"How to read this file";
"  The program is a set of words (definitions in square brackets, one capital letter each)";
"  and then a short main program. Every word has a comment above it with its stack effect";
"  ( before -- after ), the variables it uses, and a walk through its body. A comment is a";
"  string that is dropped at once, but it costs a little time each time it runs, so the";
"  loops have their explanation just before them and not inside. A slash cannot be written";
"  in a comment, so a division is spelt out.";

"Variables";
"  t  the tableau, 7 piles of 20 bytes (card j of pile p at t+20p+j, bottom first)";
"  u  the piles' sizes   v  how many of each are face down   e  how many are drawn";
"  k  the stock, 24 bytes (the top last)   s  its size   w  the waste   x  its size";
"  f  4 bytes: how many cards on the foundation of each suit   n  cards on them all";
"  d  52 bytes to shuffle   m  cards a draw takes (1 or 3)   g  1 quit, 2 won";
"  c  key   p q  source (0-6 piles, 7 waste), destination (0-6, 8 foundation)";
"  a b  the source cards that may move   i j l o y z  scratch   r  random state";
"  A card is a byte 0-51: rank (0 ace ... 12 king) + 13 * suit (0 S, 1 h, 2 d, 3 C)";

"Data layout";
"  Pile p (0 to 6) is the 20 bytes from t+20p. Its card 0 is the bottom, and it holds";
"  u[p] cards, so the top card is number u[p]-1. The first v[p] cards are face down";
"  (cards 0 to v[p]-1) and the rest are face up. A move can only touch the top and the";
"  face-up part, and when the top is taken off, the next card is turned up if it was";
"  face down. At most 6 face-down cards and 13 face-up ones fit, so 20 bytes are plenty.";
"  The stock k and the waste w are also stacks, bottom first: the top of the stock is";
"  k[s-1] (the card drawn next) and the top of the waste is w[x-1] (the last one drawn).";
"  The foundations are counts: f[suit] cards are on it, so the next one wanted is the";
"  one with rank f[suit] (0 ace). All the arrays hold bytes, read with , and written with .";
"  The array e remembers how many cards of each pile are on the screen, so that V can draw";
"  only the part of a pile that has changed.";

"Screen layout (rows and columns counted from 1)";
"  row 1   the title and the draw count       row 2   the headings Stock Waste Foundations";
"  row 3   the stock (## or blanks) at column 3, its size at 6, the waste from column 10,";
"          the foundations from column 30 (three columns for each: a card or --)";
"  row 5   the pile numbers 1-7 at column 5*i+3 for the pile i from 0 to 6";
"  rows 6+ the piles: card j of pile p is at row 6+j, column 5*p+3 (a card is two characters)";
"  column 42 onwards: the help text in rows 6 to 14, Move: in row 16, a message in row 17";
"  and Play again? in row 19";

"Routines";

"M: n d -- n mod d. The division leaves the quotient and then the remainder on top;";
"   % swaps and ; drops the quotient, which leaves the remainder.";
[M/%;]

"R: n -- a random number from 0 to n-1. A linear congruential generator in r: the next";
"   r is r*75+74 (wrapping at 16 bits). The : copies it before r! stores it. Dividing by";
"   256 and dropping the remainder keeps the upper part (about the high byte), 255& makes";
"   it a byte, and % M takes it modulo n.";
[R r@75*74+:r! 256/;255& %M]

"K: -- key. Wait for a key, stirring r while waiting. The 0 starts the stack; in the loop";
"   ; drops the old value, r is counted up, and 1? looks at the keyboard (the key, or 0";
"   if none). The : and 0= make a flag that is true while the value is 0. So it loops";
"   until a key arrives, and leaves it. The time you take to press a key seeds r.";
[K0 1(;r@1+r!1?:0=)]

"L: -- send a new line (CR then LF).";
[L13'10']

"Screen. C: clear   Y: row column -- move the cursor   E: clear to the end of the line";
"   These send ANSI escape codes. In the strings, ESC is written with the string escape";
"   for code 27. C sends ESC [ 2 J (clear) and ESC [ H (home), and E sends ESC [ K.";
"   Y takes ( row col ): it sends 27 and 91 (ESC and [), % puts the row on top and # prints";
"   it, then 59 (a semicolon), the column, and 72 (the letter H).";
[C"/[[2J/[[H"$]
[Y27'91'%#59'#72']
[E"/[[K"$]

"N: card -- rank   S: card -- suit   H: card -- flag, is it red?   Q: card -- print it";
"   The card number is rank + 13*suit, so dividing by 13 gives the suit (quotient) and the";
"   rank (remainder). N divides and drops the quotient (% ;). S drops the remainder (;).";
"   H gets the suit, copies it and tests for 1 (hearts), swaps and tests for 2 (diamonds)";
"   and ORs the two flags: the red suits are h and d. Q divides, adds the rank to the";
"   address of the string A23456789TJQK to get a character (, fetches it, ' prints it),";
"   then does the same with the suit and ShdC.";
[N13/%;]
[S13/;]
[H13/; :1= %2=|]
[Q13/ "A23456789TJQK"+,' "ShdC"+,']

"A: p j -- the address of card j of pile p   B: p -- the top card of pile p";
"   A swaps to ( j p ), multiplies p by 20, adds j and adds t. B copies p, gets the size";
"   u[p], subtracts 1 to get the top card's number, calls A and fetches the card.";
[A%20*+t@+]
[B:u@+,1- A,]

"P: p j -- move the cursor to card j of pile p, that is, to screen row j+6 and";
"   column 5*p+3. 6+ makes the row, % brings p up, 5*3+ makes the column, and Y moves.";
[P 6+ % 5*3+ Y]

"F: card pile -- flag  may the card go on that pile? (Used by G, for a move to a pile.)";
"   o is the pile, y the card, z the answer, which starts as 0.";
"   If the pile is empty (its size u[o] is 0): only a king (rank 12) may go on it.";
"   Otherwise l is the top card of the pile, and the card may go on it if its rank plus 1";
"   is the rank of l (building down) and the colours differ: y@H l@H=0= is true when the";
"   colours are not the same. The two flags are ANDed into z. The word ends with z@.";
[F o! y! 0z! o@u@+,0=( y@N12=z! 0) o@u@+,( o@B l! y@N1+ l@N= y@H l@H=0=& z! 0) z@]

"G: card -- flag  may it go to destination q?  (q is 8 for the foundation, else a pile.)";
"   y is the card. For the foundation, its rank must equal the number of cards f[suit]";
"   already on that suit's foundation. For a pile, ask F. As only one of the two blocks";
"   runs, the flag it leaves is the answer.";
[G y! q@8=( y@N y@S f@+,= 0) q@8<( y@q@F 0)]

"W: i -- card i of the source  (p is the source: 7 the waste, else a pile)";
"   For the waste (p = 7) the card is w[i]. For a pile it is card i of pile p, which A";
"   finds. As the other block does not run, a single card is left on the stack.";
[W p@7=( w@+, 0) p@7<( p@%A, 0)]

"V: p -- draw pile p where it has changed since it was last drawn.";
"   o is the pile. e[o] is how many cards were on the screen the last time, and u[o] is";
"   how many there are now. Only the part that can have changed needs drawing:";
"     o@e@+, o@u@+,     the old and the new number of cards";
"     {:}%{:}%          2dup, that is over twice: ( old new -- old new old new )";
"     >(%0);            if old > new swap, then drop the top: this is MIN of the two";
"     1- :0<(;0 0)      subtract 1, and if that is below 0 make it 0 (so MAX of it and 0)";
"     j!                j is the first card to draw: the last card that was on the screen";
"                       before and is still there (it may just have been turned face up)";
"   The same again with < gives the MAX of old and new, in y: the end of the part to draw.";
"   The loop draws the cards j up to y-1: first the cursor goes to the card (o j P), then:";
"     if j < v[o], the card is face down: print ##";
"     if j >= v[o] and j < u[o], it is face up: print the card (A finds it, Q prints it)";
"     if j >= u[o], the card has gone: print two spaces to wipe it off the screen";
"   j@y@<0=\ at the top breaks out when j is not below y. At the end e[o] = u[o], so the";
"   screen and the count agree again.";
[V o! o@e@+, o@u@+, {:}%{:}%>(%0); 1- :0<(;0 0) j!
   o@e@+, o@u@+, {:}%{:}%<(%0); y!
   1( j@y@<0=\ o@j@P j@o@v@+,<( "##"$ 0) j@o@v@+,<0= j@o@u@+,<&( o@j@A,Q 0)
      j@o@u@+,<0=( "  "$ 0) j@1+j! 1)
   o@u@+, o@e@+.]

"T: draw the stock, the waste (its top cards) and the foundations.";
"   Row 3. At column 3 the stock: ## if it has cards, two spaces if not. At column 6 its";
"   size (the space after it wipes a digit left over from a longer number). At column 10";
"   the waste: x-m is the first of the top m cards to show (m is 1 or 3), and if that is";
"   negative it becomes 0 (:0<(;0 0)). The loop prints the cards from that number up to";
"   x-1, each followed by a space (:x@<0=\ breaks when the number reaches x), then ; drops";
"   the number, and E clears the rest of the line, so the old cards disappear.";
"   At column 30 the four foundations, one for each suit i: the count c = f[i]. If it is";
"   not 0 the top card of that foundation is the card with suit i and rank c-1, which is";
"   c-1 + 13*i: Q prints it. Otherwise print --. The if-else is :( ... 0)0=( ... 0). A";
"   space follows each one.";
[T 3 3Y s@( "##"$ 0) s@0=( "  "$ 0) 3 6Y s@# " "$ 3 10Y
   x@m@- :0<(;0 0) 1( :x@<0=\ :w@+,Q " "$ 1+ 1); E
   3 30Y 0i! 1( i@f@+, :( :1- i@13*+Q 0)0=( "--"$ 0) " "$ i@1+:i! 4<)]

"Z: string -- a message, at row 17 column 42, clearing the rest of the line.";
"   (Y takes the row and the column from the stack, leaving the string for $ to print.)";
"   A message is the text of a string and then Z; the empty string clears the message.";
[Z 17 42Y $ E]

"D: draw from the stock to the waste, or turn the waste over when the stock is empty.";
"   First line: if the stock is empty (s@0=, copied by : for later), the waste is turned";
"   over into the stock: the loop for i from 0 to x-1 copies waste card x-1-i to stock";
"   position i, so the last card of the waste goes to the bottom of the stock and the first";
"   one to the top: the cards come round again in the same order as before. Then s = x,";
"   x = 0, and the";
"   saved flag is used by \ to leave the word: nothing is drawn after the turn.";
"   Second line: otherwise m is the count. While the count is above 0 and the stock is not";
"   empty (:0> s@0>& 0= is true to stop, and \ breaks): s is made 1 less, the card k[s] is";
"   taken from the top of the stock and put at the top of the waste, w[x], x gets 1 more,";
"   and the count goes down by 1. ; drops the count at the end.";
[D s@0=:( 0i! 1( i@x@<0=\ x@1-i@-w@+, i@k@+. i@1+i! 1) x@s! 0x! 0)\
   m@ 1( :0> s@0>&0=\ s@1-:s! k@+, x@w@+. x@1+x! 1- 1);]

"X: move from p to q, if a card fits. The core of the game. In the end it also draws what";
"   changed. It uses a and b for the first and last card of the source that may move, and";
"   j for the card it finds. In steps:";
"   1. Two guards. If the source is the waste (p = 7) and it is empty, or the source is a";
"      pile (p < 7) and it is empty, say so (Z) and leave (the :( ... 0)\ idiom: the flag";
"      is copied, the block prints the message and the \ leaves the word if it was true).";
"   2. Which source cards may move: a to b. For the waste only the top card, x-1, so";
"      a = b = x-1. For a pile, from its first face-up card (a = v[p]) to its top";
"      (b = u[p]-1). If the destination is the foundation (q = 8), only the top card may";
"      go, so a = b. (1b! 0a! are defaults, not used.)";
"   3. Search: j = -1 (not found), then i from a up to b: if card i of the source goes to";
"      q (W gets the card, G asks) then j = i and \ leaves the loop. So j is the DEEPEST";
"      card that fits, and the cards above it travel with it. If j is still below 0,";
"      say That doesn't go there. and leave. Otherwise clear the message (an empty string).";
"   4. Do the move. To the foundation: the suit of the card is S, f+suit is the address of";
"      the count, which is made 1 more (:,1+%. is ( addr -- ) and adds one), and n, the";
"      total at home, goes up by 1. To a pile: for i from j to b, copy card i of the source";
"      to position u[q] of pile q (A finds the address) and add one to u[q].";
"   5. Take the cards off the source. The waste: x goes down by 1. A pile: its size u[p]";
"      becomes j. If this leaves the pile with nothing but face-down cards (u[p] = v[p])";
"      and there is one, turn the top one up by subtracting 1 from v[p]. Then draw the";
"      source pile again with V.";
"   6. Draw the destination pile (if it is a pile) with V, draw the rest with T, and if";
"      n is 52 all the cards are home: g = 2, the game is won.";
[X p@7= x@0=&:( "The waste is empty."Z 0)\ p@7< p@u@+,0=&:( "That pile is empty."Z 0)\
   "Step 2: the cards that may move.";
   1b! 0a! p@7=( x@1-:a!b! 0) p@7<( p@v@+,a! p@u@+,1-b! 0) q@8=( b@a! 0)
   "Step 3: find the deepest one that fits.";
   -1j! a@i! 1( i@b@>\ i@W G:( i@j! 0)\ i@1+i! 1)
   j@0<:( "That doesn't go there."Z 0)\ ""Z
   "Step 4: put it on the foundation, or move the cards to the pile.";
   q@8=( j@W S f@+ :,1+%. n@1+n! 0)
   q@8<( j@i! 1( i@b@>\ i@W q@ q@u@+,A . q@u@+:,1+%. i@1+i! 1) 0)
   "Step 5: take them off the source, turn a card up if need be, and redraw it.";
   p@7=( x@1-x! 0)
   p@7<( j@p@u@+. p@u@+, p@v@+,= p@v@+,0>&( p@v@+:,1-%. 0) p@V 0)
   "Step 6: redraw the destination pile and the top row, and see if the game is won.";
   q@8<( q@V 0) T n@52=( 2g! 0)]

"O: key -- the pile 0-6 for keys 1-7, the waste 7 for w, the foundation 8 for f, or -1";
"   z is the key. The -1 is put on the stack first. If the key is above 48 and below 56";
"   (the characters 1 to 7), ; drops the -1 and z@49- gives 0 to 6. If the key is 119 (w)";
"   the answer is 7, and 102 (f) gives 8. Anything else leaves -1.";
[O z! -1 z@48> z@56<&( ; z@49- 0) z@119=( ; 7 0) z@102=( ; 8 0)]

"I: shuffle and deal: a pile p of p+1 cards, the top one face up; the rest is the stock";
"   Shuffle: d is filled with the cards 0 to 51 in order (the first loop). Then the";
"   Fisher-Yates shuffle: i counts down from 51 to 1, j is a random number from 0 to i";
"   (R takes i+1), and the cards d[i] and d[j] are swapped: i@d@+, and j@d@+, fetch them,";
"   the first . stores d[j] at d[i], and the second . stores d[i] at d[j].";
"   Deal: j is the position in the shuffled deck and p the pile. The inner loop puts the";
"   next card in slot i of pile p (A finds it) for i from 0 to p, that is, p+1 cards. The";
"   loop flag p@>~ means i <= p (the test i > p, inverted by ~). Then pile p has p+1 cards";
"   (u[p]), p of them face down (v[p]), and 0 on the screen yet (e[p]).";
"   The stock is the 24 cards from position 28 (the tableau used 1+2+...+7 = 28 cards).";
"   Then the stock has 24 cards (s), the waste none (x), none are at home (n and f).";
[I 0i! 1( i@i@d@+. i@1+:i! 52<)
   51i! 1( i@1+R j! i@d@+, j@d@+, i@d@+. j@d@+. i@1-:i! 0>)
   0j! 0p! 1( 0i! 1( j@d@+, p@i@A. j@1+j! i@1+:i! p@>~) p@1+ p@u@+. p@p@v@+. 0p@e@+.
      p@1+:p! 7<)
   0i! 1( i@28+d@+, i@k@+. i@1+:i! 24<) 24s! 0x! 0n! 0i! 1( 0i@f@+. i@1+:i! 4<)]

"U: draw the table: clear the screen, print the title with the draw count m, the headings,";
"   the pile numbers (row 5) and the piles (with V: since e is 0 for every pile, V draws";
"   all of each), then T draws the top row. The help text is at column 42.";
[U C 1 3Y "KLONDIKE   draw "$ m@# 2 3Y "Stock   Waste"$ 2 30Y "Foundations"$
   0i! 1( 5 i@5*3+Y i@1+# i@V i@1+:i! 7<) T
   6 42Y "d     draw from the stock"$ 8 42Y "A move is a source, then a destination:"$
   9 42Y "w     the waste"$ 10 42Y "1-7   a pile"$ 11 42Y "f     the foundation"$
   12 42Y "So w 3, or 2 f, or 7 1."$ 14 42Y "q     quit"$]

"Main program";

"Allocate the arrays (_ gives the address of the bytes): the tableau 7*20 = 140, u v and e";
"7 each, the stock and the waste 24 each, f 4, and the 52 cards to shuffle. Start r at 0.";
140_t! 7_u! 7_v! 7_e! 24_k! 24_w! 4_f! 52_d! 0r!

"The introduction: clear the screen and explain the game.";
C "KLONDIKE"$ L L "Build the four foundations up by suit from the ace. The tableau builds down"$ L
"in alternate colours, and only a king goes on an empty pile. d draws; a move is"$ L
"a source and then a destination: w the waste, 1-7 a pile, f the foundation."$ L L

"The outer loop is one game on each pass, and repeats while you answer y at the end.";
"The inner loop asks for the draw count until it is 1 or 3: K waits for a key (stirring r,";
"which seeds the shuffle), :32>(:' 0) echoes it if it is printable, L ends the line,";
"48- turns the digit into a number and stores it in m, and m@1= m@3=| 0= is true while";
"m is not 1 or 3. (In the prompt, two slashes print as one.)";
1( 1( "Draw one card at a time, or three? (1//3) "$ K :32>(:' 0) L 48- m! m@1= m@3=| 0=)
   "Shuffle and deal, draw the table, and set g to 0 (0 playing, 1 quit, 2 won).";
   I U 0g!
   "The move loop. Prompt Move: at row 16, column 42, with E clearing the old text. 0?";
   "waits for a key and 32| makes it lower case (the digits are not changed by it).";
   1( 16 42Y "Move: "$ E 0? 32| c!
      "d (100): draw, redraw the top row, and clear any message. q (113): g = 1 ends it.";
      c@100=( D T ""Z 0) c@113=( 1g! 0)
      "Anything else is the source key. O turns the key into p: if p is 0 to 7 (valid, and";
      "not 8, as the foundation is not a source) show it in row 16 at column 48 (w, or the";
      "pile number), then ' to ' and read the second key into q. The move is tried if q is";
      "valid (above -1), is not the waste (q is not 7) and is not the same as p.";
      c@O p! p@-1> p@8<&( 16 48Y p@7=( "w"$ 0) p@7<( p@1+# 0) " to "$ 0? 32| O q!
         q@-1> q@7=0=& q@p@=0=&( X 0) 0)
   g@0=)
   "The game is over, by quitting or winning. Clear the Move line, say if it was won,";
   "and ask again: y starts another game.";
   16 42Y E g@2=( 17 42Y "All 52 are home. You win!"$ E 0)
   19 42Y "Play again? (y//n) "$ E K 121=)
C "Bye."$ L
