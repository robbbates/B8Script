"DUNGEON for B8Script. A small rogue-like: find the stairs > and go deeper.";
"Move with w a s d (walk into a monster to fight it), p drinks a potion, q quits.";
"Pick up gold $, potions !, a sword (slash symbol: +3 damage, 10 hits) and armor ]";
"(-2 damage, 12 hits).";

"Hide the cursor while it runs (/` in a string hides it): delete the next line";
"for a terminal that can't hide its cursor.";
"/`"$

"How it works, in short: the level is one block of bytes, a map of 40 x 14";
"characters that is also what gets printed (the map is drawn by printing its";
"bytes). Monsters are kept apart from the map: two small tables hold their cells";
"and hit points, and they are drawn on top as M. Each turn is: draw, read a key,";
"do your move (U) or drink (Y), then every live monster acts (V). The level is";
"made at random by G each time you take the stairs; all random choices use R.";

"Variables";
"  m  map, 40 x 14 bytes: # wall . floor $ gold ! potion (slash) sword ] armor > stairs";
"  z  your cell   a  monster cells (6 cells)   e  monster hit points (0 = dead)";
"  h  your hit points (up to 20)   g  gold   l  level   p  potions";
"  w  sword: hits left   u  armor: hits left   s  message   c  key   d  step";
"  q n t v j k x y f i  scratch   r  random state";

"Data layout. A cell number is an offset into the map: cell = row*40 + column,";
"rows 0 to 13 and columns 0 to 39, 560 cells in all, one byte each. The map has";
"no separate wall tests: the outer ring is always wall. One step left or right is";
"-1 or +1 in the cell number, one step up or down is -40 or +40.";
"a and e are two tables of 6 cells (16-bit, 2 bytes each), one entry per monster:";
"  monster number k has its cell at a + 2*k and its hit points at e + 2*k.";
"Hit points 0 (or less) mean dead: that entry is then ignored everywhere.";
"s holds the address of a message string (0 for none) that D shows once, on screen";
"row 16 (rows count from 0 at the top).";

"Routines";

"M: ( n d -- n mod d ).  Division (the slash) leaves quotient and remainder (remainder on";
"top); % swaps and ; drops the quotient. Used by R, D and W.";
[M/%;]

"R: ( n -- 0..n-1 ).  A random number. r is a linear congruential generator:";
"  r = r * 75 + 74 (16-bit wrap), stored back with r! (the : keeps a copy).";
"  Dividing by 256 (and dropping the remainder) keeps r div 256, about the top";
"  byte, the best bits, and 255& cuts it to 0-255. % brings n on top and M takes that byte mod n.";
[R r@75*74+:r! 256/;255& %M]
"   Exactly: for a state of 0 to 32767 this is its high byte. Above that the state reads as";
"   negative and the division rounds toward zero, so the byte comes out one more than the";
"   high byte (255 turning into 0) unless the low byte is 0. It is still a number 0 to 255.";

"K: ( -- key ).  Wait for a key, stirring r while waiting, so the random numbers";
"  differ from game to game. The 0 is a dummy that the first pass drops. Each";
"  pass: drop the old value, add 1 to r, then 1? looks for a key (the key, or 0";
"  at once). : 0= makes a flag, true while no key came, which repeats the loop.";
"  The key stays on the stack when the loop ends.";
[K0 1(;r@1+r!1?:0=)]

"L: ( -- ).  New line: CR (13) then LF (10).";
[L13'10']

"A: ( n -- |n| ).  Absolute value: copy, test for negative, and if so negate";
"  with -1*.";
[A:0<( -1* 0)]

"N: ( n -- sign )  its sign: 1, 0 or -1. Each test copies n (:), and if the test";
"  holds the ( body drops n (;) and leaves 1 or -1 instead. A positive n becomes 1,";
"  which then fails the second test (1 < 0 is false) and is kept as it is.";
[N :0>( ; 1 0) :0<( ; -1 0)]

"F: ( -- cell )  a random floor cell. Loops until the map byte at a random place";
"  is 46 (a dot, floor); the ~ turns the equality flag round, so the loop goes";
"  on while the cell is NOT floor. t is the cell, row 14R (0-13) * 40 + 40R.";
[F 1( 14R40* 40R+ t! t@m@+,46= ~) t@]

"G: ( -- )  make a level: fill the map, place the items and monsters, place you.";
[G
   "Step 1, the map. i is the cell number 0 to 559; i div 40 gives row and column.";
   "Dividing i by 40 leaves quotient (row) and remainder (column); x! takes the column and";
   "y! the row. The cell character starts as 46 (floor). If x is 0 or 39, or y is";
   "0 or 13, it is on the ring, so the 46 is dropped and 35 (wall) takes its place.";
   "Then 100R17< is true 17 times in 100: that cell also becomes a wall at random.";
   "Finally the character is stored at map cell i and i counts up to 560.";
   0i! 1( i@40/ x! y!
     46 x@0= x@39=| y@0=| y@13=|( ; 35 0) 100R17<( ; 35 0)
     i@m@+. i@1+:i! 560<)
   "Step 2, items. F picks a random floor cell; m@+ makes its address; . stores.";
   "The stairs (62 is >) first, then 5 gold (36 is $): the 5 is a counter on the";
   "stack, 1-: lowers it and copies it for the loop test; the final 0 is dropped.";
   62F m@+.  5 1( 36F m@+. 1-:) ;
   "Two potions (33 is !), then each with a 1 in 2 chance (2R is 0 or 1): a sword";
   "(47 is the slash symbol) and armor (93 is ]).";
   33F m@+. 33F m@+.
   2R( 47F m@+. 0) 2R( 93F m@+. 0)
   "Step 3, monsters k = 0 to 5. Each gets a random floor cell (stored in cell k of";
   "table a) and hit points l + 3 + 0 to 2 (stored in table e), so they get tougher";
   "with the level. k@2*a@+! writes the 2-byte cell at a + 2*k.";
   0k! 1( F k@2*a@+! l@3+ 3R+ k@2*e@+! k@1+:k! 6<)
   "Last, your own cell z, and clear the screen (/{). D will draw everything.";
   F z! "/{"$]

"P: ( cell -- )  move the cursor to the screen position of a map cell.";
"  Dividing by 40 gives row and column on the stack. Screen rows and columns count";
"  from 0, and map rows start on screen row 1 (row 0 is the status line), so the";
"  cell is first moved one row down (40+): then the division gives the screen row";
"  and column, just as /~ in a string wants them ( row column addr ).";
[P 40+40/"/~"$]

"O: ( cell -- n )  1 + the number of the monster standing there, or 0 if none.";
"  t is the cell asked about, v the answer, j the monster being checked.";
"  A monster counts if its hit points are above 0 (e) and its cell (a) equals t.";
"  The flags are combined with & so no branch is needed. If two live monsters";
"  share a cell the one with the higher number is returned.";
[O t! 0v! 0j!
   1( j@2*e@+@0> j@2*a@+@t@=&( j@1+v! 0) j@1+:j! 6<)
   v@]

"Z: ( cell -- flag )  can a monster step there? Not a wall, no monster already";
"  there (O gives 0), and not your cell. Each test is made into a flag and the";
"  three are combined with &. The ~ turns an equality flag into a not-equal flag.";
[Z t! t@m@+,35= ~ t@O 0= & t@z@= ~ &]

"D: ( -- )  draw the screen: status line, map, monsters, you, message, help line.";
"  The status and help lines end with /}, which clears the rest of the line, so";
"  a shorter text never leaves old letters behind.";
[D
   "Cursor home (row 0, column 0 under /~), then the status line: /l /h /g /p print";
   "level, hit points, gold and potions. 20 is the maximum hit points, shown as HP";
   "then your hit points, a slash and 20 (a double slash in a string prints one).";
   0 0"/~DUNGEON  Level /l  HP /h//20  Gold /g  Potions /p  Sword "$
   "Sword and armor: when hits are left (non-zero) print the number, else none.";
   w@( w@# 0) w@0=( "none"$ 0) "  Armor "$ u@( u@# 0) u@0=( "none"$ 0) "/}"$ L
   "The map: print all 560 bytes. After every 40th byte (i mod 40 is 0) start a";
   "new line. The count i@1+i! needs no copy: the loop test fetches i again.";
   0i! 1( m@i@+,' i@1+i! i@40M0=( L 0) i@560<)
   "Monsters: for each live one, move the cursor to its cell and print M (77).";
   0k! 1( k@2*e@+@0>( k@2*a@+@P 77' 0) k@1+:k! 6<)
   "You: the cursor to cell z and print @ (64), over the map character there.";
   z@P 64'
   "Row 16: the message, if any (s is its address, printed with $), then clear it";
   "(0s!) so that it shows only once. Row 17: the key help.";
   16 0"/~"$ s@( s@$ 0) "/}"$ 0s!
   17 0"/~w a s d move   p drink a potion   q quit/}"$]

"Y: ( -- )  drink a potion: up to 8 hit points back.";
"  With no potions (p is 0) set the message and leave the word at once: the :";
"  copies the flag for the ( block, and the flag left over feeds the guard exit.";
[Y p@0=:( "You have no potions."s! 0)\
   "One potion used; 8 hit points more, but never above the limit of 20.";
   p@1-p! h@8+h! h@20>( 20h! 0) "You drink a potion and feel better."s!]

"B: ( -- damage )  the damage you deal: 2 to 5 (4R is 0 to 3, plus 2). With a";
"  sword (w not 0) add 3 and use up one hit; when the last hit goes, say it broke.";
[B 4R2+ w@( 3+ w@1-w! w@0=( "Your sword breaks!"s! 0) 0)]

"U: ( -- )  your move, from the key in c. Moves, fights, picks things up, or does";
"  nothing if the key is not a direction (then it is still a turn for monsters).";
[U
   "Step d: 97 is a (left, -1), 100 is d (right, +1), 119 is w (up, -40) and";
   "115 is s (down, +40). Other keys leave d at 0, and the guard exit ends U.";
   0d! c@97=( -1d! 0) c@100=( 1d! 0) c@119=( -40d! 0) c@115=( 40d! 0)
   d@0=\
   "q is the target cell. O gives 1 + the monster there (or 0) into v.";
   z@d@+ q!
   q@O v!
   "If a monster is there (v not 0; the : ( uses a copy, and the v left on the";
   "stack makes the guard exit that ends U after the block): fight. k = v - 1 is";
   "its number. Its hit points become hit points - B (the damage). The : keeps a";
   "copy of the result for the test; k@2*e@+! stores it.";
   v@:( v@1-k! k@2*e@+@ B - :k@2*e@+!
               "Dead if the new hit points are below 1: say so and take 5 gold. The";
               "flag is copied for ( and the ~ ( then runs if it is NOT dead; it says";
               "you hit it, unless a message such as the sword breaking is there.";
               1<:( "You slay the monster!"s! g@5+g! 0)~( s@0=( "You hit the monster."s! 0) 0) 0)\
   "No monster. A wall at the target: stay (35 is #, the guard exit ends U).";
   q@m@+,35=\
   "Move there. Then, by what is at the new cell (read from the map):";
   "36 gold: 10 to 19 more (10R + 10); 33 potion; 47 sword; 93 armor; 62 stairs.";
   "In each case the cell is cleared to 46 (floor), except the stairs, which";
   "start a new level: level + 1 and G makes the map, items and monsters.";
   q@z!
   z@m@+,36=( "You find gold!"s! g@10R10++g! 46z@m@+. 0)
   z@m@+,33=( "You find a potion."s! p@1+p! 46z@m@+. 0)
   z@m@+,47=( "You find a sword: 10 hits."s! 10w! 46z@m@+. 0)
   z@m@+,93=( "You find armor: 12 hits."s! 12u! 46z@m@+. 0)
   z@m@+,62=( "You go down the stairs."s! l@1+l! G 0)]

"H: ( -- damage )  the damage a monster deals: 1 to l div 3 + 2, so it grows with";
"  the level. R takes the range, 1+ puts it at least 1. With armor (u not 0): take";
"  2 off (2- subtracts), keep it at 0 or more (the same clamp idiom as in A and";
"  N), and use up one hit; when the last one goes the armor falls apart.";
[H l@3/;2+R1+ u@( 2- :0<( ; 0 0) u@1-u! u@0=( "Your armor falls apart!"s! 0) 0)]

"W: ( -- )  monster k acts. q is its cell. x and y are the distance from the";
"  monster to you: columns (cell mod 40) and rows (cell div 40) of you minus it.";
"  q is its cell before it moves; n is the cell it considers stepping to.";
[W k@2*a@+@q! z@40M q@40M- x! z@40/; q@40/;- y!
   "Next to you, even diagonally (both distances below 2: A is the absolute value):";
   "it hits. H gives the damage; h@%- subtracts it from h. The message appears";
   "only if none is set. The adjacency flag stays on the stack and the guard exit";
   "ends W, so an adjacent monster never moves.";
   x@A2< y@A2<&:( H h@%-h! s@0=( "A monster hits you!"s! 0) 0)\
   "Too far (the sum of the distances above 9): it does nothing. Else it steps";
   "towards you. First along x if x is not 0: n is its cell plus the sign of x (N).";
   "If Z says that cell is free, move there (store n in its cell) and set f.";
   x@A y@A+ 9>\
   0f! x@( x@N q@+ n! n@Z( n@k@2*a@+! 1f! 0) 0)
   "If it has not moved (f is 0) and y is not 0 (the flag f@0= is all ones, so";
   "& leaves y as the test), try along y: the sign of y times 40, plus its cell.";
   f@0= y@&( y@N40* q@+ n! n@Z( n@k@2*a@+! 0) 0)]

"V: ( -- )  every live monster acts, monster 0 to 5.";
[V 0k! 1( k@2*e@+@0>( W 0) k@1+:k! 6<)]

"Main program";
"Seed r with 0 (K stirs it later). Allocate the map (560 bytes) and the two tables";
"of six 2-byte cells (12 bytes each); _ returns each block's address, kept with !.";
0r!
560_m! 12_a! 12_e!
"Start with 20 hit points, no gold, level 1, no message, no potions, sword or armor.";
20h! 0g! 1l! 0s! 0p! 0w! 0u!
"The title and help screen. K waits for a key and so also stirs r; ; drops it.";
"/{DUNGEON"$ L L "Find the stairs > and go as deep as you can."$ L
"w a s d move; walk into an M to fight it. p drinks a potion."$ L
"! potion   // sword: +3 damage, 10 hits   ] armor: -2 damage, 12 hits"$ L L "Press a key."$ L K;
"Make the first level.";
G
"Main loop, one pass per turn: draw; read a key into c; 112 is p: drink (Y);";
"otherwise (not p) your move (U); then V lets the monsters act. The loop goes on";
"while you are alive (h above 0) and the key was not q (113). The ~ turns the";
"equality flag round and & joins the two conditions.";
1( D K c! c@112=( Y 0) c@112= ~( U 0) V h@0> c@113=~& )
"The end: draw the final screen, go to row 18, print how it ended: died on level";
"(h below 1) or left on level (h above 0, you quit), then the level and the gold.";
D 18 0"/~"$
h@1<( "You died on level "$ 0) h@0>( "You left on level "$ 0) "/l with /g gold."$ L

"Show the cursor again (/|): delete the next line too if the one at the start was deleted.";
"/|"$
