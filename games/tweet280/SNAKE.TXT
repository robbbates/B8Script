"SNAKE for B8Script, in a tweet. Steer with , (left turn) and . (right turn), eat";
"the *, and don't bite yourself. The board is 64 by 16 and wraps at the edges.";
"The score, shown under the board, is how many you have eaten. The snake is the";
"O at its head, with the body behind it, and it grows when it eats.";
"Needs a console with the screen escapes.";

"Variables";
"  g  the map: a heap block of 1024 bytes (64 * row + column); 0 is empty, 128 is";
"     food, and a body cell holds the direction the head left it by, 1 to 4";
"     (the head's cell holds 1 until it leaves)   m  the mask 1023, for wrapping";
"  r  random state   i  the head's place   j  the tail's place";
"  d  the direction 0 to 3 (right, down, left, up)   l  score";

"Clear the screen and hide the cursor.";
"/{/`"$

"The map: note the heap top in g, then allocate 1024 bytes one at a time, storing";
"  a 0 in each. m is 1023.";
0_g!1024 1(0 1_.1-:);1023m!

"P ( pos -- ): put the cursor at pos: row pos // 64, column the remainder. The";
"  string prints nothing; //~ takes the two numbers.";
[P64/"/~"$]

"R ( -- pos ): a random place, 0 to 1023. r = r * 75 + 74, stored back; then";
"  r / 64 (quotient only) is masked with m.";
[Rr@75*74+:r!64/;m@&]

"F ( -- ): place the food. Draw random places until one is empty (loop: drop the";
"  last try, take another, read its map byte). Store 128 there, draw a * there,";
"  and print the score at row 17, which changes only when it has to.";
[F0 1(;R:g@+,):g@+128%.P"*"$17 0"/~score /l"$]

"D ( dir -- step ): the cell step for a direction: +1, +64, -1, -64. n&1 gives 1";
"  or 64, and 1 - (n&2) gives +1 or -1.";
[D:1&63*1+%2&1%-*]

"The start: random state, head, tail, direction and score all 0; then the first";
"  food.";
0r!0i!0j!0d!0l!F

"The game is one loop, a move a pass. Each move:";
"  1. Wait up to 99 ms for a key; step the generator (R, result dropped), so the";
"     next food depends on when you ate. . gives -1 and , gives +1: d minus that,";
"     and 3&, is the new direction.";
"  2. Store d + 1 in the head's cell (the way it leaves).";
"  3. Move the head: i plus the step, masked. Read the map byte of the new cell.";
"     If its low three bits are set, the head hit the body: break out (\).";
"  4. If the byte is not 0, it is food: add 1 to l and place new food. The tail";
"     stays, so the snake grows. If it is 0 (empty), the tail moves: erase it with";
"     a blank, read its cell for the direction, clear the cell, and step j.";
"  5. Mark the head's cell with 1, move the cursor there and print O. Go on.";
1(99?R;:46=%44=-d@%- 3&:d!
  1+i@g@+.
  d@Di@+m@&:i!g@+,
  :7&\
  :(l@1+l!F0)
  0=(j@:P32':g@+:,1-D%0%.+m@&j!0)
  i@:g@+1%.P79'1
);

"The end: after a break the map byte is left on the stack; drop it. Then row 17,";
"  show the cursor again, and the score.";
17 0"/~/|score /l"$
