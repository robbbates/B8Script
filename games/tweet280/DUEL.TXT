"DUEL for B8Script, in a tweet. Tron for two on a 64 by 16 arena that wraps at the";
"edges. You are O, the computer is X, and you start facing each other. Each bike";
"leaves a wall behind it. The first to hit a wall (or the other bike) loses.";
"Keys: , turns left, . turns right. Needs a console with the screen escapes.";
"The line under the arena says who is who, then shows WIN, LOSE or DRAW.";

"Variables";
"  g  the map, a heap block of 1024 bytes; a cell is 64 * row + column, and a bike";
"     leaves a 1 in every cell it enters   i d  your place and direction";
"  j e  the computer's place and direction   p q  your crash and its crash: the";
"     map byte of the cell each bike moved into (nonzero is a hit)";
"  Directions 0 1 2 3 are right, down, left, up (so +1 turns right).";

"Clear the screen, hide the cursor, and write the key line at row 17 with one";
"  string: //{ homes the cursor, then the //~ takes the 17 and 0 pushed before it.";
17 0"/{/`/~O you, X me: , left . right"$

"The map: note the heap top in g, then allocate 1024 bytes one at a time, storing";
"  a 0 in each.";
0_g!1024 1(0 1_.1-:);

"D ( dir -- step ): the cell step for a direction. n&1 gives 1 or 64; 1 - (n&2)";
"  gives +1 or -1; the product is +1, +64, -1 or -64.";
[D:1&63*1+%2&1%-*]

"S ( pos dir ch -- pos' old ): move a bike one cell and draw it. The character is";
"  parked; the new place is pos + step, wrapped by 1023&. Its row and column";
"  (place // 64, and the remainder) go to //~ in an empty string, which moves the";
"  cursor; then the character is printed. Last, the map cell is read and a 1 is";
"  stored in it: old is what was there before.";
[S{D+1023&:64/"/~"$}':g@+:,%1%.]

"A ( dir -- byte ): the map byte one cell ahead of the computer's bike, in dir.";
[ADj@+1023&g@+,]

"The start: you at 520 (row 8, column 8) heading right; the computer at 556";
"  (row 8, column 44) heading left.";
520i!0d!556j!2e!

"The game is one loop, a move a pass; it goes on until a bike has crashed.";
"  Each move:";
"  1. Wait up to 99 ms for a key. . gives -1 and , gives +1; d minus that, and";
"     3&, is your new direction (a right turn or a left turn, or the same).";
"  2. Move and draw your bike (O is 79). The map byte it moved into is p.";
"  3. The computer looks ahead in its direction e. If the cell is taken, it turns";
"     right (e + 1); if that is taken too, it turns the other way (e + 3), and";
"     that turn is not looked at. It sees only one cell ahead.";
"  4. Move and draw its bike (X is 88). The map byte it moved into is q.";
"  5. The loop flag is (p or q) = 0: go on while neither bike has crashed.";
1(99?:46=%44=-d@%- 3&:d!
  i@%79S%i!p!
  e@A(e@1+3&e!0)
  e@A(e@2+3&e!0)
  j@e@88S%j!q!
  p@q@|0=
)

"The end: row 17, clear that line and show the cursor again. Push WIN; if you";
"  crashed, swap in LOSE; if both did, swap in DRAW. Print what is left.";
17 0"/~/}/|"$
" WIN"p@(;" LOSE"0)q@p@&(;" DRAW"0)$
