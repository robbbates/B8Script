"REVERSI for B8Script (Othello), on 8 x 8, against the computer. X moves first.";
"A move puts a disc on an empty cell so that, in at least one of the eight directions,";
"one or more of the other side's discs lie in a straight line between it and one of";
"your own: all those discs are flipped to your side. A player with no such move";
"passes. The game ends when neither side can move; the one with more discs wins.";
"Keys: w a s d move the cursor, space or Return plays the cell under it. Or type a";
"column letter and a row digit, as d3, which plays at once. m marks every cell you";
"can play, q resigns. You choose who goes first. The screen must be 80 x 24.";
"The computer takes a corner when it can, keeps off the cells next to an empty";
"corner, likes the edges, and otherwise flips as many discs as it can.";

"How to read the notes. ( a b -- c ) is a stack effect: what a word takes and leaves,";
"the top of the stack on the right. [X ... ] defines word X; calling X runs its body.";
"Every comment is a string that is dropped at once (the semicolon), so it does nothing.";
"Short words that run many times keep their notes just before them, not inside, since a";
"comment still takes a little time to read where it runs (only in the .TXT: b8strip.py";
"takes every comment out of the .B8S).";

"The plan in short:";
"  1. The board b is 10 x 10 bytes: the 8 x 8 cells inside a border, so a walk in";
"     any direction stops at the border with no edge tests.";
"  2. Z walks the eight directions from a cell and counts the discs a move there";
"     would flip. With f set it flips them too. A move is legal if Z gives more than 0.";
"  3. W, the computer, scores every legal move and plays the best. U reads your move.";
"  4. s is the board as the screen shows it. V draws only the cells that differ, so a";
"     move redraws the new disc and the flipped ones, and the marks of m come and go.";
"  5. The main loop gives the turn to each side in turn; a side that cannot move";
"     passes, and when neither can, the game is over.";

"Variables";
"  b  the board, 100 bytes   s  what the screen shows of it, 100 bytes";
"  y  your side (1 X or 2 O)   z  whose turn it is   p o  the side to move, the other";
"  c  a cell   f  flip the discs, not just count   n  discs flipped (Z's answer)";
"  j d t k  used by Z: direction number, step, a cell walked to, discs in a line";
"  u v  the cursor's column and row (1 to 8); at the end, your discs and mine";
"  l  a column letter waiting for its digit";
"  q  key   m  the move chosen   h  show the marks   x  game over   g  you resigned";
"  e  the best score so far (W)   a i w  scratch";

"Data layout. Cell c is row*10 + column, with row and column 1 to 8 on the board, so";
"a1 is 11 and h8 is 88. Rows 0 and 9 and columns 0 and 9 are the border. A byte holds";
"0 empty, 1 X, 2 O, or 3 in the border, which matches neither side. The other side of";
"p is 3-p. The eight directions are steps from a cell to its neighbours:";
"  -11 -10  -9";
"   -1   .   1";
"    9  10  11";
"kept in the string BCDLNVWX as characters 77 more than the step (B is 66, X is 88),";
"so they print as plain text. On the screen the cell is at line row+2, column 2*col+2,";
"counted from 0.";
"A byte of s holds 0 1 2 as b does, or 4 for a mark: the characters . X O ? + at";
"those places in a string are what is drawn. s starts at 9, which matches nothing.";

"Routines";

"L: new line (CR then LF).";
[L`']

"Screen. Y: ( row column -- ) move the cursor, both counted from 0   E: clear to the";
"end of the line. Short names for the console codes `@ and `_, which are used often.";
"(The screen is cleared and the cursor homed with `^ itself.)";
[Y`@]
[E`_]

"O: ( i -- c )  the cell for i from 0 to 63, row by row. Dividing by 8 gives the row";
"   and the column from 0, column on top: 1+ makes it 1 to 8, the swap brings the row";
"   up to be made 1 to 8 and times 10, and + adds the column.";
[O 8/ 1+ % 1+ 10* +]

"P: ( c -- )  move the cursor to cell c: dividing by 10 gives row and column (on";
"   top); the column becomes 2*col+2 (:+ doubles), the row row+2, row first for Y.";
[P 10/ :+2+ % 2+ % Y]

"I: ( c -- )  print the name of cell c, as D3: the column plus 64 is its capital";
"   letter, then the row as a number.";
[I 10/ 64+' #]

"Z: ( c -- n )  how many discs side p would flip by playing at the empty cell c, and";
"   if f is set, flip them. For each direction j: d is its step. t walks from c while";
"   it finds the other side's discs (o), k counting them; the walk stops at the first";
"   cell that is not o: one of p's own, an empty cell or the border. Only if it is p's";
"   own disc, and k is above 0, do those k discs count (and get flipped).";
"   The flip walks from c again: the stack holds the cell and the count; each pass";
"   steps the cell on by d and stores p in it (:b@+ p@%. keeps the cell), and 1-:";
"   counts down. The ;; drops the cell and the spent count.";
[Z c! 0n! 0j! 1( j@"BCDLNVWX"+,77- d! c@d@+t! 0k!
      1( t@b@+,o@=0=\ t@d@+t! k`+; 1)
      t@b@+,p@= k@0>&( n@k@+n!
         f@( c@ k@ 1( %d@+ :b@+ p@%. % 1-: );; 0) 0)
   j`+ 8<) n@]

"A: ( -- flag )  can side p move at all? OR together Z of every empty cell: not 0 if";
"   any move flips something.";
[A 0 0i! 1( i@O:c! b@+,0=( c@Z| 0) i`+ 64<)]

"N: ( p -- count )  how many discs side p has on the board. A comparison is -1 when";
"   true, so subtracting it adds 1.";
[N a! 0 0i! 1( i@O b@+, a@= - i`+ 64<)]

"Q: ( c -- flag )  is c a corner (11 18 81 88)? Its row and column must each be 1 or";
"   8: :1=%8=| tests the column, the swap brings the row up for the same test.";
[Q 10/ :1=%8=| % :1=%8=| &]

"H: ( c -- flag )  is c on an edge? Its row or its column is 1 or 8.";
[H 10/ :1=%8=| % :1=%8=| |]

"X: ( c -- flag )  is c next to an empty corner? Look at its eight neighbours: one";
"   that is a corner (Q) and empty (byte 0) sets the flag, ORed over the eight.";
"   :Q keeps the neighbour for the byte test; the swap brings it back up.";
[X t! 0 0j! 1( j@"BCDLNVWX"+,77- t@+ :Q %b@+,0=& | j`+ 8<)]

"V: draw every cell whose screen byte in s differs from what it should show. w is";
"   what it should show: the board byte, or 4 (a mark) if the marks are on (h), the";
"   cell is empty and side p could play there. Where w differs from s, the cursor";
"   goes to the cell, the character for w is printed and s takes w.";
[V 0i! 1( i@O:c! b@+,w! h@ w@0=&( c@Z( 4w! 0) 0)
      w@ c@s@+, =0=( c@P ".XO?+"w@+,' w@ c@s@+. 0)
   i`+ 64<)]

"B: show the counts of discs, X and O, on row 5 of the screen (from 0), in the notes.";
[B 5 29Y "X "$ 1N# "   O "$ 2N# E]

"G: ( c -- )  play side p's move at c: put the disc there and flip, with f set for";
"   the one call of Z, whose count is dropped.";
[G :b@+ p@%. 1f! Z; 0f!]

"W: ( -- )  the computer's move, for side p, into m. Every empty cell with a legal";
"   move (Z, the discs flipped, is a) gets a score: a, plus 100 on an edge, plus 3000";
"   on a corner, minus 2000 next to an empty corner. flag n& is n when the flag is";
"   true and 0 when it is false, so the bonuses need no ifs. So a corner always wins,";
"   a cell next to an empty corner is played only when there is nothing else, an";
"   edge beats any cell inside, and among those the most flips win. The score times";
"   8, plus a random 0 to 7 (8`,), breaks ties at random. The best so far is e, its";
"   cell m. The largest score, 3000+100+20 times 8 plus 7, still fits in a cell.";
[W -32000e!
   "For each cell: if it is empty and the move flips something (a, from Z), score";
   "it, and keep it in m if it beats e (the score is copied for the test and the";
   "store; the ; drops it).";
   0i! 1( i@O:c! b@+,0=( c@Z a! a@(
         a@ c@H 100&+ c@Q 3000&+ c@X 2000&- 8* 8`,+
         :e@>( :e! c@m! 0); 0) 0)
   i`+ 64<)]

"U: ( -- )  read your move into m, or set g if you resign. The loop goes on until m";
"   is a legal cell or g is set. The cursor sits on the cell u,v. A key above 64 (a";
"   letter) is folded to lower case; Return, space and digits are left as they are.";
"   a is the cell asked for by this key, or 0.";
"   A digit 1 to 8 just after a letter a to h (l is that column) asks for that cell,";
"   and moves the cursor there. Then l is the column of this key if it is a letter a";
"   to h, or 0: so a column letter waits for one key only. w a s d move the cursor";
"   (a and d are column letters too: they move it and wait for a digit as well).";
"   Space (32) or Return (13) asks for the cell under the cursor. m shows the marks;";
"   q resigns. A cell asked for must be empty and flip something.";
[U 0m! 0l! 1(
      "The cursor on its cell, a key (letters folded), nothing asked for yet.";
      u@ v@10*+P 0? :64>( 32| 0) q! 0a!
      "A digit 1 to 8 (49 to 56) after a column letter: that cell, cursor on it.";
      q@48> q@57<& l@&( q@48-v! l@u! v@10* u@+ a! 0)
      "A letter a to h (97 to 104) waits for its digit.";
      0l! q@96> q@105<&( q@96- l! 0)
      "w s a d move the cursor.";
      q@119= v@1>&( v`-; 0) q@115= v@8<&( v`+; 0)
      q@97= u@1>&( u`-; 0) q@100= u@8<&( u`+; 0)
      "Space or Return: the cell under the cursor. m: the marks. q: resign.";
      q@32= q@13=|( v@10* u@+ a! 0)
      q@109=( 1h! V 0) q@113=( 1g! 0)
      "A cell asked for: legal (empty, and Z above 0) makes it the move and clears";
      "row 15 (from 0); else row 15 says no.";
      a@( 15 4Y a@b@+,0= a@Z0>&:( a@m! E 0)0=( "You can't play there."$ E 0) 0)
   m@0= g@0=&)]

"D: draw the board: the title, the column letters above and below, the row digits";
"   at both sides, the notes on the right. The cells are drawn by V, since s is 9.";
[D `^ 0 4Y "REVERSI"$ 2 4Y "A B C D E F G H"$ 11 4Y "A B C D E F G H"$
   1i! 1( i@2+ 1Y i@# i@2+ 20Y i@# i`+ 9<)
   3 29Y "You are "$ ".XO"y@+,' ", I am "$ ".XO"3y@-+,' "."$
   7 29Y "w a s d move, space or Return plays,"$ 8 29Y "or type a letter and a digit, as d3."$
   9 29Y "m marks your moves, q resigns."$]

"Main program";

"Make the board and its screen copy (100 bytes each).";
100_b! 100_s!
"Title screen and instructions. 0? waits for a key (the wait stirs the random seed);";
"the ; drops the key.";
`^ "REVERSI"$ L L "Put a disc where it shuts a line of mine between it and one of your own:"$ L
"those discs all turn to your side. Most discs at the end wins."$ L L "Press a key."$ 0?;

"Outer loop: one game per pass. Ask who goes first: y (121) makes you X (1), who";
"moves first, and anything else makes you O (2). Every byte of b is 3 (border), then";
"the 64 cells are cleared; s is all 9 so V draws every cell. The four starting discs:";
"O on d4 and e5 (44, 55), X on e4 and d5 (45, 54). X moves first (z is 1). f is 0,";
"so Z only counts until G sets it for a move.";
1( `^ "Do you want to go first? (y//n) "$ 2y! 0? 121=( 1y! 0)
   0i! 1( 3i@b@+. 9i@s@+. i`+ 100<) 0i! 1( 0i@O b@+. i`+ 64<)
   2 44b@+. 2 55b@+. 1 45b@+. 1 54b@+.
   1z! 0x! 0g! 0h! 0f! 4u! 3v! D V B
   "Turn loop. p is the side to move, o the other. If p can move (A): your move is";
   "read by U, mine is found by W, saying Thinking... while it looks. Unless you";
   "resigned, G plays it, the marks go off, V redraws and B counts. Then the other's";
   "move is reported.";
   1( z@p! 3z@-o!
      A:( z@y@=( U 0) z@y@=0=( 14 4Y "Thinking..."$ E W 0)
         g@0=( m@G 0h! V B z@y@=0=( 14 4Y "I play "$ m@I "."$ E 0) 0) 0)
      "If p cannot move: if the other side can, p passes and says so; if neither";
      "can, the game is over (x).";
      0=( 3z@-p! z@o! A:( z@y@=( 15 4Y "You have no move, so you pass."$ E 0)
            z@y@=0=( 14 4Y "I have no move, so I pass."$ E 0) 0)
         0=( 1x! 0) 0)
      "Hand the turn over: 3-z. Go on while the game is not over and you play on.";
      3z@-z!
   x@0= g@0=&)
   "The game is over. Count the discs: u yours, v mine (N uses i, so not i), and say";
"who won on row 17 (from 0).";
   y@N u! 3y@-N v! 17 4Y g@( "You resign. "$ 0)
   g@0=( u@v@>( "You win "$ u@# " to "$ v@# "."$ 0)
      u@v@<( "I win "$ v@# " to "$ u@# "."$ 0) u@v@=( "A draw, "$ u@# " each."$ 0) 0)
   E 18 4Y "Play again? (y//n) "$ E
0? 121=)
`^ "Bye."$ L
