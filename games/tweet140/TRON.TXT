"TRON for B8Script. A light cycle that never stops, leaving a wall behind it.";
"Keys: , turns left and . turns right. Hit your own trail and the game ends, with the";
"  number of steps as the score. The arena is 64 wide and 16 high and wraps round at";
"  its edges. It needs a console with the cursor escapes (//~).";
"Variables: g the map (1024 bytes, one per cell, 0 empty, 1 trail), i the cycle's";
"  place (row * 64 + column), d its direction: 0 right, 1 down, 2 left, 3 up.";
"The score lives on the stack, under the loop's flag.";

"Start: clear the screen and hide the cursor, then get the map: 1024 bytes taken from";
"  the heap one at a time, each set to 0. 520 is row 8, column 8. Direction right.";
"/{/`"$
0_ g!
1024 1( 0 1_ . 1- : ) ;
520i! 0d!

"Main loop: one step per pass.";
"  The turn: 99? waits up to 99 ms for a key (this is the pace). The key . gives -1 and";
"  , gives +1 (:46=%44=-). Taking that off d turns right or left, and 3& keeps d in 0..3.";
"  The step: (d&1)*63 + 1 is 1 or 64, and 1 - (d&2) is 1 or -1. Their product is +1,";
"  +64, -1 or -64 for d = 0, 1, 2, 3. It is added to i and 1023& wraps the arena";
"  round, so no other bounds test is needed. i is kept.";
"  Draw an O at the new place: i divided by 64 leaves the row and the column for //~.";
"  Then read the old cell and mark it 1 in one go (the old value stays under) and add 1";
"  to the score. The loop continues while the old value was 0.";
0 1( 99? :46= % 44= - d@ % - 3& :d!
     :1& 63* 1+ % 2& 1% - *
     i@+ 1023& :i!
     :64/ "/~O"$
     g@+ :, % 1% . % 1+ % 0= )

"The end: put the cursor on row 17, show it again and print the score.";
17 0"/~/|score "$
