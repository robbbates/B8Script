"COPTER for B8Script, in a tweet. A cave scrolls up the screen, one line a frame.";
"Your craft, H, is pulled to the right; any key pushes it left. Keep it inside";
"the walls, |. The cave narrows from 12 to 5 wide as you go. On a crash it says";
"CRASH! and gives the number of lines you flew. Any key is the only control.";

"Variables";
"  p  the column of the left wall (a random walk)   w  the cave's width";
"  x  the craft's place in quarter-columns (its column is x // 4)";
"  v  its speed in quarter-columns a frame, -4 to 3   t  lines flown, the score";
"  r  random state, stirred by the keys";

"Print the title and a new line.";
"COPTER: any key pushes left/M/J"$

"S ( n -- ): print n spaces; none if n is under 1.";
[S1(:1<\32'1- 1);]

"The start: wall at column 9, craft at place 56 (column 14), still, score 0.";
9p!56x!0v!0t!0r!

"The game is one loop, a line a pass, that ends when the craft is out of the cave.";
"  Each line:";
"  1. Step the generator r. Take its high byte, 0 to 31, and move p by one step:";
"     -1 if the byte is below p, then +1 if the state r is negative (a coin";
"     flip), so p moves by -1, 0 or 1 and wanders.";
"  2. The width w is 12 less t // 50, but not under 5.";
"  3. Wait up to 99 ms for a key, and add it to r. A key sets v to -4.";
"  4. v goes up by 1, to at most 3 (gravity); the craft moves by v.";
"  5. Its column minus p must be above 0 and under w, or the loop breaks (\).";
"  6. Draw the line: p spaces and a wall, the craft's column minus p minus 1";
"     spaces and H, then the spaces up to the right wall at p + w, a wall, and";
"     a new line.";
"  7. t goes up by 1.";
1(r@75*74+:r!:0<%256/;31&p@<%-p@+p!
  12t@50/;-:5<(;5 0)w!
  99?:r@+r!(-4v!0)
  v@1+:3>(;3 0):v!x@+:x!
  4/;p@-:0>%w@<&0=\
  p@S"|"$
  x@4/;p@- 1-S"H"$
  p@w@+x@4/;- 1-S"|/M/J"$
  t@1+t!1
)

"The end: say so, with the score t.";
"CRASH! score /t"$
