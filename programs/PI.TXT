"PI for B8Script: up to 250 digits of pi, by the spigot of Rabinowitz and Wagon (1995).";
"The digits come out one at a time, from 16-bit arithmetic only. pi = 2 + 1/3 (2 + 2/5";
"  (2 + 3/7 (2 + ...))): an array of l cells starts at 2 each, and every pass multiplies";
"  it by 10 and carries from the right, so the carry out of the left is the next digit.";
"  A digit of 9 can still turn into 10 later, so 9s are held (k of them, after the held";
"  digit p) until a digit that isn't 9 settles them.";
"The largest number a pass makes grows with the number of digits: 250 digits (and the 5";
"  more worked out, so that the last ones printed are settled) stay below 32768.";
"Written for speed, with the algorithm unchanged: in the inner loop the carry q stays";
"  on the stack, and y walks down the array, two bytes a cell, instead of being worked";
"  out from i for every cell.";
"Variables: n digits wanted, m worked out, l cells, a the array (on the heap), j passes";
"  left, i the cell, q the carry, y a cell's address, k held 9s, p the held digit (-1:";
"  none yet), c digits printed, v used by N.";

"N ( -- n ): read a number typed with echo; it ends at the first key that is not a digit.";
[N0v!1(0?:48<\48-:#v@10*+v!1);v@]
"M ( n d -- n mod d ).";
[M/%;]
"E ( d -- ): print digit d, if fewer than n are out: a point after the 3, a space every";
"  10 decimals and a new line every 50.";
[E c@n@<:{( c@1>( c@1- 10M0=( c@1- 50M0=:{( "/M/J  "$ 0)}0=( 32' 0) 0) 0)
  48+' c@0=( 46' 0) c@1+c! 0)}0=( ; 0)]

"PI: digits of pi. How many (1-250)? "$ N :1<(;1 0) :250>(;250 0) n! 13'10'
n@5+m! m@10*3/;1+l!
l@:+_a!
0i! 1( 2a@i@:++! i@1+:i! l@<)
0c! 0k! -1p!

"m passes, or fewer once n digits are out.";
m@j! 1(
  "the cells from the right: x = 10 a(i) + q i; a(i) = x mod 2i-1; q = x / 2i-1.";
  "  q is on the stack; y is the address of a(i), the cell numbered from 1.";
  l@i! a@l@:+2-+y! 0 1(
    i@* y@@10*+ i@:+1-/ y@!
    y@2-y! i@1-:i!)
  "the carry is the next digit, or 10 to add 1 to the held ones";
  10/ a@! q!
  q@9=( k@1+k! 0)
  q@10=( p@1+E k@( 1( 0E k@1-:k!) 0) 0p! 0)
  q@9<( p@0<0=( p@E 0) q@p! k@( 1( 9E k@1-:k!) 0) 0)
  j@1-:j! c@n@<&)
p@E 13'10'
