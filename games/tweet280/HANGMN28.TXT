"HANGMAN for B8Script, in a tweet. Guess the six-letter word a letter at a time;";
"six misses and you lose. Eight words, all from the machine room. The first key";
"you press picks the word. Keys: letters, in either case.";

"Variables";
"  w  the address of the chosen word in the word string (6 letters, no NUL)";
"  b  a 7-byte heap block: the word so far, _ for the letters not found, then a";
"     quote, so that b@$ prints it as a string";
"  m  misses   u  letters not found yet   c  the key (upper case)";
"  f  true if the key was in the word";

"Print the title, and count while waiting for a key. The count & 7, times 6, is";
"  the word's offset in the word string. The eight words follow one another:";
"  STACKS BINARY MEMORY CURSOR SYNTAX PARSER BUFFER KERNEL.";
"HANGMAN, 6 misses. Key"$0 1(1+1?0=)7&6*"STACKSBINARYMEMORYCURSORSYNTAXPARSERBUFFERKERNEL"+w!

"The word so far: allocate 7 bytes in b, fill the first six with _ (95), and end";
"  with a quote (34) as the string's end. Then no misses, and six letters to find.";
7_b!6 1(1-:b@+95%.:);34b@6+.0m!6u!

"The game is one loop, a guess a pass. Each pass:";
"  1. Print a new line, the word so far, and the misses out of 6 (////6 is a slash";
"     and a 6) with a ? prompt.";
"  2. Wait for a key; 95& makes it upper case. It goes in c.";
"  3. For each of the six places, if the word's letter is c: if the place in b is";
"     still _, one less letter is left (u falls by 1); put c in b; set f.";
"     A letter found before counts as found again, at no cost.";
"  4. If f is not set, add a miss.";
"  5. Go on while letters are left (u above 0) and there are fewer than 6 misses.";
1("/M/J"$b@$" /m//6 ?"$
  0?95&c!
  0f!6 1(1-:w@+,c@=(:b@+:,95=u@+u!c@%.-1f!0):);
  f@0=(m@1+m!0)
  u@0>m@6<&
)

"The end: print a new line and the whole word, a letter at a time, from w. Then";
"  won, or lost if letters are still hidden (u is not 0).";
"/M/J"$w@6 1(%:,'1+%1-:);;
" won"u@(;" lost"0)$
