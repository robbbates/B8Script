"KINGDOM for B8Script, in a tweet: Hamurabi in miniature. You rule ten years.";
"Each year type how much grain to feed your people, and press Return. Each";
"person needs 20. If fewer than half the people are fed, you are overthrown.";
"What is left over is planted, 10 a head, on at most 999 acres. The score, on a";
"line of its own at the end, is your population.";

"Variables";
"  p  population   g  grain   y  the year, 1 to 10   r  random state, stirred by";
"  what you type   q  true if you were overthrown this year";

"N ( -- n ): read a number. Add each key's digit (key - 48) to ten times what";
"  came before, echoing it, until Return (13). The loop breaks out on Return, so";
"  the Return is dropped by the ; after the loop.";
[N0 1(0?:13=\:'48-%10*+1);]

"M ( n -- n or g ): n, but no more than the grain g.";
[M:g@>(;g@0)]

"Print the title. Start with 99 people, 4999 bushels, random state 0, year 1.";
"KINGDOM: eat 20, plant 10 each"$99p!4999g!0r!1y!

"The game is one loop, a year a pass; it ends after year 10, or when you break";
"  out by being overthrown. Each year:";
"  1. Print the year, the people and the grain, and ask feed? Read N.";
"  2. Mix the number typed into r. Limit it to the grain with M. Take it from g.";
"  3. The people fed are that over 20. q is true if they are under half of p, and";
"     then the loop breaks (\). The fed count is left on the stack, and dropped";
"     after the loop.";
"  4. Fed minus p is the surplus. If positive, half of it (in people) is added;";
"     if negative, the people it falls short by are lost. This gives the new p.";
"  5. The grain to plant is p * 10, limited to the grain left and to 999.";
"  6. Step the generator and take its high byte mod 4, plus 1: k, 1 to 4. Print";
"     the year's yield as k + 1. Grain gains planted * k, which is the yield with";
"     the seed taken back out (the seed was never taken from g).";
"  7. Next year; the loop flag is y under 11.";
1("/M/J/y: pop /p grain /g feed? "$
  N
  :r@+r!
  M
  :g@%-g!
  20/;
  :p@2/;<:q!\
  p@-:0>(2/;0)
  p@+:p!
  10*M:999>(;999 0)
  r@75*74+:r!256/;3&1+
  :" yield "$1+#*g@+g!
  y@1+:y!11<
)

"The end: if you broke out, drop the fed count and say Overthrown. Then a new";
"  line and the score, the population p.";
q@(;" Overthrown"$0)
"/M/Jscore /p"$
