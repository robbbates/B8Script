"LIGHTS OUT for B8Script. A 5 by 5 board of lights; turn them all off.";
"Each cell has a key, a to y, left to right and top to bottom. A capital letter is a";
"  light that is on, a small one is off. Pressing a cell's key toggles it and its four";
"  neighbours. All 25 are on at the start (a hard one: the best solution is 15 presses).";
"  Capital keys work too. The board is drawn in place, with the number of presses so";
"  far beside its bottom row. It needs a console with the cursor escapes (//{ and //~).";
"Variables: g the grid, s the count of lit cells made while drawing.";
"The grid is 7 rows of 6 bytes, with a dead column on the right (it is also the left";
"  border of the next row) and dead rows above and below, so every neighbour of a cell";
"  is just 1 or 6 bytes away, with no edge tests. The press count lives on the stack.";

"F ( addr -- ): flip the byte at addr between 0 and 1 (fetch, XOR 1, store).";
[F :, 1^ % .]
"T ( addr -- ): toggle the cell at addr and its four neighbours: F on the cell, then on";
"  the cells 1 before, 1 after, 6 before and 6 after.";
[T :F :1- F :1+ F :6- F 6+ F]
"Q ( n -- addr ): the address of cell n, 0 to 24. n 5// gives the row and the column,";
"  and row * 6 + column, plus the grid g, plus 6 (past the dead row on top).";
[Q 5/ % 6* + g@+ 6+]

"Start: clear the screen once, then get the grid: 50 bytes of heap, each set to 1, so";
"  every light starts on. The border starts on too, but it is never shown or counted.";
"/{"$
0_ g!
50 1( 1 1_ . 1- : ) ;

"Main loop: one press per pass, with the press count (0 to start) on the stack.";
"  Draw: clear s, then for each cell n from 0 to 24 (n on the stack): :5// and //~ move";
"  the cursor to its row and column; then fetch its byte b (Q ,) and add it to s. The";
"  letter is 97 + n, less 32 * b, so a lit cell is a capital. Draw it and add 1 to n.";
"  Afterwards drop n, print a space and the press count.";
"  If s is 0 no light is left on, and \ ends the loop.";
"  Else read a key with 0? (it is not echoed). 1- 31& turns it into a place from 0 to 31,";
"  so a stray key can never write outside the grid; Q makes its address, T toggles.";
"  Add 1 to the press count and go round again.";
"  Most keys fall on a cell. A few (z to ~, Z to ^ and : to >) fall on the dead row";
"  below and toggle one cell of the bottom row alone. Space, @, grave, ? and _ and DEL";
"  toggle nothing that shows, but count as presses.";
0 1( 0s!
     0 1( :5/ "/~"$
          :: Q , :s@+ s!
          32* - 97+ '
          1+ :25< ) ;
     32' :#
     s@0= \
     0? 1- 31& Q T
     1+ 1)

"The end: drop the press count.";
;
