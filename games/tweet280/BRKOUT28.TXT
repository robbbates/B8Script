"BREAKOUT for B8Script, in a tweet. Three rows of 32 bricks, a paddle, a ball.";
"Keys: j moves the paddle left, l moves it right (2 columns a frame). Break the";
"bricks and keep the ball in play. The score, under the paddle, is the number of";
"bricks broken. The game ends when the ball falls to the paddle's row.";
"Needs a console with the screen escapes.";

"Variables";
"  g  the map: a heap block of 736 bytes, 32 per row, 23 rows; 1 is a brick";
"  x y  the ball, in screen columns 1 to 32 and rows 0 to 20 (its map cell is";
"       y * 32 + x - 1)";
"  d e  the ball's step in x and in y, each 1 or -1   s  score";
"  p  the paddle's column, even, 0 to 28 (the bar is the 4 characters at p + 2)";

"Z ( addr -- ): negate the cell at addr. The bounces use it: dZ and eZ.";
[Z:@0%-%!]

"Clear the screen.";
"/{"$

"The map: note the heap top in g, then allocate 736 bytes one at a time, storing";
"  a 0 in each (the allocate-and-clear trick).";
0_g!736 1(0 1_.1-:);

"The bricks: for n from 95 down to 0, cell n + 32 (rows 1 to 3) gets a 1 in the";
"  map, and a # is drawn for it at row (n + 32) // 32, column (n + 32) mod 32 + 1.";
96 1(1-:32+:g@+1%.32/1+"/~#"$:);

"The start: the ball at row 11, column 11 moving right and up (d 1, e -1); the";
"  score and the paddle at 0.";
11:y!x!1d!-1e!0s!0p!

"The game is one loop, a frame a pass; it goes on while the ball's row is under 20.";
"  Each frame:";
"  1. Wait up to 250 ms for a key. j gives -1 and l +1; doubled, that is the";
"     step. If the new paddle place is 0 to 29 (the unsigned test 32767& 30<";
"     catches a negative one too), it is stored; otherwise it is dropped.";
"  2. Draw the paddle at row 19 with two blanks on each side, which wipe its trail.";
"  3. Wipe the ball's old place with a blank.";
"  4. Bounce off the side walls (x is 1 or 32) and the top (y is 0).";
"  5. Move the ball, and look at the map cell it moved into. A brick there is";
"     cleared, the ball turns in y and the score goes up. The brick is never";
"     redrawn over: next frame the ball's old cell is wiped, which wipes it.";
"  6. If the ball is on row 19 and over the paddle ((x - p + 2) // 4 is 1), it";
"     turns in y.";
"  7. Draw the ball at its row and column, and the score at row 21 column 0, with";
"     one string and its two //~ places. The loop flag is y under 20.";
1(250?:106=%108=-:+p@+:32767&30<(:p!0);
  19p@"/~  ====  "$
  y@x@"/~ "$
  x@1=x@32=|(dZ0)
  y@0=(eZ0)
  x@d@+x!y@e@+:y!32*x@+1-g@+
  :,(:0%.eZs@1+s!0);
  x@p@- 2+4/;1=y@19=&(eZ0)
  21 0y@x@"/~o/~score /s"$
  y@20<
)
