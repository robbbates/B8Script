"LIGHTS OUT for B8Script. A 5 x 5 grid of lights. Pressing a light switches it and its";
"four neighbours (up, down, left and right; fewer at an edge): a lit one goes dark and";
"a dark one lights. Put every light out. Keys: w a s d move the cursor, space presses";
"the light under it, n deals a new puzzle at the same level, q quits.";
"Each puzzle is made by pressing random lights on a dark grid, so it can always be";
"solved: press the same lights again. Level 1 is made with 3 presses, and each level";
"adds 2 more. The moves you take are counted. The screen must be 80 x 24.";

"How to read the notes. ( a b -- c ) is a stack effect: what a word takes and leaves,";
"the top of the stack on the right. [X ... ] defines word X; calling X runs its body.";
"Every comment is a string that is dropped at once (the semicolon), so it does nothing.";
"Short words that run many times keep their notes just before them, not inside, since a";
"comment still takes a little time to read where it runs (only in the .TXT: b8strip.py";
"takes every comment out of the .B8S).";

"The plan in short:";
"  1. Each row of the grid is one cell holding 5 bits, one per light: bit x is the";
"     light in column x, 1 lit, 0 dark. So the whole grid is five numbers.";
"  2. A press is three exclusive ORs (^): one on the row above, one on the row below";
"     and one on the row itself, with a mask of three bits. See P.";
"  3. The grid has a spare row above and below the five real ones, which soak up the";
"     XORs of a press on the top or bottom row, so P needs no edge tests.";
"  4. h is a copy of the rows as the screen shows them. V XORs each row with its copy:";
"     the bits that come out set are exactly the lights that changed, and only those";
"     are drawn again.";
"  5. The grid is out when the five rows ORed together give 0.";

"Variables";
"  g  the grid, 7 cells: row 0 spare, rows 1 to 5 the lights, row 6 spare";
"  h  the rows as drawn on the screen, 7 cells (1 to 5 used)";
"  x y  the cursor, column and row, each 0 to 4   n  key";
"  l  level   p  random presses used to make the puzzle   m  moves taken";
"  o  how the turn loop ended: 0 playing, 1 all out, 2 q, 3 n (a new puzzle)";
"  a  play on   b j  used by P   d i j  used by V   u v  used by D";

"Data layout. The grid g is 7 cells (14 bytes) from the heap; row j is the cell at";
"g + 2j, and the lights of screen row y (0 to 4) are in row y+1. In a row, light";
"x (0 to 4, left to right) is bit x, worth 1 2 4 8 16. Bits 5 and up are always 0.";
"Rows 0 and 6 are never shown and never tested: they only take the presses that";
"spill over the top and bottom edges.";

"Routines";

"Random numbers: n`, gives a number from 0 to n-1. The interpreter stirs its seed while";
"the game waits for a key, so each run deals different puzzles.";

"L: new line (CR then LF).";
[L`']

"Y: ( row column -- )  move the cursor. Rows and columns count from 0.";
"   (The screen is cleared with `^ and a line is cleared to its end with `_ .)";
[Y`@]

"B: ( x -- bit )  the bit for column x: 1 doubled x times (1 2 4 8 16).";
"   1% puts a 1 under x. Each pass: :1<\ leaves the loop once the count x is below 1;";
"   %:+% swaps the bit to the top, doubles it (:+) and swaps it back under the count;";
"   1- counts down. At the end the ; drops the spent count and the bit is left.";
[B 1% 1( :1<\ %:+% 1- 1);]

"A: ( j -- addr )  the address of row j of the grid: g + 2j (:+ doubles j).";
[A :+g@+]

"P: ( x y -- )  press the light at column x, row y (0 to 4): switch it and its four";
"   neighbours. j is the grid row, y+1; b is the light's bit.";
"   The rows above and below hold one neighbour each, in the same column: XOR their";
"   cells with b. The pattern :@ b@^ %! fetches a row (keeping its address), XORs it";
"   and stores it back: the swap puts the address on top for the store.";
"   The row itself needs three bits: the light and the ones on each side. b*7 is";
"   b + 2b + 4b, the bit and the two above it; halving that (2/;) moves all three one";
"   place down, so they sit at x-1, x and x+1. At column 0 the bit below 1 has nowhere";
"   to go: it becomes the remainder of the halving, which the ; drops. At column 4 the";
"   top bit lands on bit 5, off the grid, and 31& clears it. So the edges need no tests,";
"   and the spare rows 0 and 6 do the same job for the top and bottom.";
[P 1+ j! B b!
   j@1-A :@ b@^ %!
   j@1+A :@ b@^ %!
   j@A :@ b@7* 2/; 31& ^ %!]

"Q: ( x y -- )  move the cursor onto light x,y. On the screen (counting from 0), row y";
"   is line 5+2y and column x is the middle of the three characters 28+4x to 30+4x, so";
"   column 29+4x.";
"   The swap brings x up to be worked out, then puts the line first for Y.";
[Q 2*5+ % 4*29+ Y]

"D: ( x y -- )  draw light x,y: [*] if lit, a dot between spaces if dark. Q goes to";
"   its middle and 8' (backspace) steps back one to the start of its three characters.";
"   The row (y+1) ANDed with the bit for x is not 0 when the light is lit. The if-else";
"   :( lit 0)0=( dark 0) copies that test: one copy opens the lit block, the other is";
"   turned round by 0= for the dark one.";
[D v! u! u@ v@Q 8' v@1+A@ u@B& :( "[*]"$ 0)0=( " . "$ 0)]

"V: draw every light that changed since the last draw, and bring h up to date.";
"   For each row j from 1 to 5, d is the row XOR its copy in h (at h + 2j): a bit of d";
"   is set exactly where the light changed. For each column i, if d has its bit, D";
"   draws light i,j-1. Then the row is copied into h. A press changes at most five";
"   lights, so a press redraws at most five.";
[V 1j! 1( j@A@ j@:+h@+@ ^ d!
      0i! 1( d@ i@B&( i@ j@1- D 0) i`+ 5<)
      j@A@ j@:+h@+! j`+ 6<)]

"W: ( -- flag )  true when every light is out: OR the five rows into one value on the";
"   stack, starting from 0, and test it for 0.";
[W 0 1j! 1( j@A@| j`+ 6<) 0=]

"S: make a puzzle: clear all 7 rows, then make p presses at random cells.";
"   The presses can undo one another (the same cell pressed twice is no press at all),";
"   and in rare cases they leave the grid dark. Then W is true and the whole thing is";
"   done again, so the player never gets a puzzle that is already solved.";
"   The count of presses is kept on the stack: 1-: ) counts it down to 0, and the ;";
"   after the loop drops it.";
[S 1(
      "All seven rows dark, then p presses at random columns and rows.";
      0j! 1( 0 j@A! j`+ 7<)
      p@ 1( 5`, 5`, P 1-: );
   "Again if that left them all dark.";
   W)]

"F: draw the screen for a new puzzle: the title, the keys, a frame with 25 dark lights";
"   inside, from line 4 to line 14 and columns 26 to 48 (counting from 0), and the";
"   number of presses used. The lights rows are lines 5, 7, ... 13 (j odd: j@1& keeps";
"   the low bit, and the copy turned round by 0= picks the blank lines between).";
"   The lines between are blank inside the frame. Then every row of h is set to 0, which is what the screen now";
"   shows, so the V that follows draws just the lit ones.";
[F `^ 1 26Y "LIGHTS OUT"$ 2 13Y "w a s d move    space presses    n new puzzle    q quits"$
   4 26Y "+---------------------+"$
   5j! 1( j@26Y j@1&:( "|  .   .   .   .   .  |"$ 0)
      0=( "|                     |"$ 0) j`+ 14<)
   14 26Y "+---------------------+"$
   17 26Y "Made with "$ p@# " presses."$
   1j! 1( 0 j@:+h@+! j`+ 6<)]

"G: show the level and the moves taken, on line 16 (from 0).";
[G 16 26Y "Level "$ l@# "    Moves "$ m@# `_]

"Main program";

"Make the grid and its screen copy (7 cells each).";
14_g! 14_h!
"Title screen and instructions. 0? waits for a key; the ; drops the key.";
`^ "LIGHTS OUT"$ L L "Each press switches a light and the four next to it. Put every light out."$ L
"w a s d move the cursor, space presses, n deals a new puzzle, q quits."$ L L
"Press a key."$ 0?;

"Start at level 1. The outer loop deals one puzzle per pass: p is the number of";
"random presses, 2l+1, so 3 at level 1 and 2 more at each level. S makes the puzzle,";
"F draws the screen, V the lit lights and G the counts. The cursor starts in the middle.";
1l!
1( l@2*1+p! S 0m! 0o! F V G 2x! 2y!
   "Turn loop: one key per pass. Q puts the terminal's cursor on the light, 0? waits";
   "for a key and 32| folds it to lower case. w a s d move the cursor if it stays";
   "inside 0 to 4: the key test and the edge test are joined with &.";
   1( x@ y@Q 0? 32| n!
      n@119= y@0>&( y`-; 0) n@115= y@4<&( y`+; 0)
      n@97= x@0>&( x`-; 0) n@100= x@4<&( x`+; 0)
      "Space: press the light under the cursor, count the move, redraw what changed.";
      n@32=( x@ y@P m`+; V G 0)
      "Then see how the loop ends: all out, or q (113), or n (110) for a new puzzle.";
      W( 1o! 0) n@113=( 2o! 0) n@110=( 3o! 0)
   o@0=)
   "After the puzzle. a decides whether the outer loop deals another: always after n,";
   "and after a win if the answer is y (121), which also goes up a level. q ends it.";
   0a! o@3=( 1a! 0)
   o@1=( 19 26Y "All out in "$ m@# " moves! Next level? (y//n) "$ `_
      0? 121=( l`+; 1a! 0) 0)
a@)
L L "Bye."$ L
